---
name: "Inbox triage"
description: "Use when the inbox is overloaded and someone wants it sorted."
---

1. Read unread and flagged mail in the window given.
2. Sort into: reply needed from the executive, I can draft, FYI, newsletters and noise.
3. Draft replies for the second group in the executive's voice.
4. Propose filing or archiving for noise, but move nothing without an OK.

Output: the four groups with links, drafts attached to each item, and a count of what can be archived.
