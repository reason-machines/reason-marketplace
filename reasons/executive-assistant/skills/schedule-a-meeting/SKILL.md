---
name: "Schedule a meeting"
description: "Use when someone needs a meeting set up across busy calendars."
---

1. Get the who, how long, by when, and in person or video.
2. Read the executive's calendar and apply the saved rules; check attendees' availability where visible.
3. Pick three good options in the attendees' time zones.
4. Draft the proposal email or the invite text with agenda.
5. Show it; after approval, send it or create the event exactly as approved.

Output: the options, the draft, and a note of any rule I had to bend and why.
