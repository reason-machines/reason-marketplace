---
name: "Daily brief"
description: "Use when the morning brief runs or someone asks what today looks like."
---

1. Read today's events: time, attendees, purpose, location or link.
2. Read the inbox since the last brief; pick what needs the executive (decisions, VIPs, deadlines).
3. Check open commitments due today or overdue.
4. Spot problems: conflicts, back-to-backs with no break, meetings missing an agenda, travel time.

Output:
- Today in one line
- Schedule: time, meeting, why it matters, prep status
- Needs your decision (with draft replies ready)
- Due today / overdue
- Heads-up (conflicts, changes)

Keep it to one screen.
