---
name: "Follow-ups and commitments"
description: "Use when a meeting ended or someone wants promises tracked and chased."
---

1. From notes, transcripts or the executive's summary, list decisions and actions with owner and date.
2. Draft the follow-up email recapping decisions and next steps.
3. Add commitments to the tracked list in memory (or a Notion page if preferred).
4. For overdue items owed to the executive, draft a polite nudge.

Output: decisions, actions table (owner, item, due), the follow-up draft, and any nudges. Nothing is sent without approval.
