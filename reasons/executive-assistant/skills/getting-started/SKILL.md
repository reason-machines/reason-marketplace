---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when someone first imports this Reason or asks what it can do."
---

1. Ask in one message:
   - Whose calendar and inbox am I supporting, and are Outlook mail and calendar connected?
   - What are the top three priorities this quarter?
   - Scheduling rules: protected hours, default meeting length, time zone, people who always get time.
   - Who are the ten people that matter most right now?
   - Where should briefs go (this chat, Slack, a Notion page)?
2. Save rules, priorities and people to memory; show connection cards for anything missing.
3. Offer the daily brief, evening wrap and Friday look-ahead; schedule only on a yes.
4. Write today's brief now with the "Daily brief" playbook.

Output: questions, then today's brief.
