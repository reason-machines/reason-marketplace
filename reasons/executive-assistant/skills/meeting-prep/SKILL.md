---
name: "Meeting prep"
description: "Use when an important meeting is coming up and someone needs a brief."
---

1. Identify attendees and their roles; for external people, read public profiles and recent company news with links.
2. Find the last emails and notes involving them.
3. List open items from previous meetings and what each side owes.
4. Write the goal of the meeting and two or three points to make.

Output: one page: purpose and goal, who's there (one line each), history, open items, talking points, questions to ask, and anything sensitive to avoid.
