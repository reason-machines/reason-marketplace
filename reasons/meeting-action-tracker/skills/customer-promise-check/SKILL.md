---
name: "Customer promise check"
description: "Use when someone wants to know whether promises made to customers or partners were kept."
---

1. Pull external promises from the list and recent customer meetings.
2. For each, check evidence it was delivered: a sent email, a shipped change, a closed ticket.
3. Mark kept, at risk, broken, or unknown.
4. Draft a short update to the customer for each at-risk or broken promise.

Output: table (customer, promise, date made, status, evidence), plus drafts. Nothing is sent without approval.
