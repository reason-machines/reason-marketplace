---
name: "Extract actions"
description: "Use when there are meeting notes or transcripts to turn into decisions and actions."
---

1. Read the full transcript or notes, not only the summary.
2. List decisions (what was decided, by whom).
3. List actions: owner, task, due date, quoted source line with timestamp if present.
4. List promises to people outside the company separately.
5. List open questions.

Output:
- Decisions
- Actions table (owner, task, due, source quote)
- External promises
- Open questions
- Missing owners or dates

Never assign an owner the transcript doesn't support.
