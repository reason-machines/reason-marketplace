---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when someone first imports this Reason or asks what it can do."
---

1. Ask in one message:
   - Where does support come in (Plain, Intercom, Pylon, email, Slack)?
   - Where are your docs or help center, and where do bug tickets go (Linear, GitHub)?
   - What counts as urgent, and what is your target first-reply time?
   - Anything I must never say or promise (dates, discounts, roadmap)?
   - Paste two or three replies you're proud of so I can match your voice.
2. Save the rules and voice to memory; show connection cards for missing tools.
3. Offer the queue sweep, waiting-on-us check and weekly summary; schedule only on a yes.
4. Run "Triage the queue" now on the open conversations, or on threads they paste.

Output: questions, then the first triage list with drafts.
