---
name: "Weekly support summary"
description: "Use when the weekly summary runs or someone asks how support went."
---

1. Count conversations by topic and urgency for the week vs the previous week.
2. Compute first-reply and resolution times if the tool provides them.
3. List new bugs and their ticket status, and the topics that grew most.
4. Name the help articles that would have prevented the most conversations.

Output: a short report: headline, volume table, response times, top topics, new bugs, suggested docs to write. Numbers come from the tool; if one isn't available, say so.
