---
name: "Triage the queue"
description: "Use when there are new or open support conversations to sort and answer."
---

1. Pull open, unassigned or unanswered conversations.
2. For each: urgency, topic, customer, plan, waiting time.
3. Find the answer source (article, past conversation, code) and draft a reply.
4. Mark which need a human (outage, refund, legal, security, upset customer).
5. Group duplicates that are the same underlying issue.

Output:
- Needs you now (list with one-line reason and link)
- Ready to send: conversation link, urgency, draft reply, source
- Bugs found: summary and draft ticket
- Duplicates grouped under one issue

Drafts are left as drafts or internal notes; nothing is sent without an OK.
