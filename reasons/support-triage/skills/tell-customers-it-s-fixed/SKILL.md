---
name: "Tell customers it's fixed"
description: "Use when a bug ships and customers who reported it should hear back."
---

1. Find the ticket and every support conversation linked to it.
2. Confirm the fix is actually released (release notes, merged PR, deploy) before drafting anything.
3. Draft a short personal reply per customer: what was wrong, that it's fixed, anything they need to do.
4. Show the list and drafts; send nothing until approved, then send only the approved ones.

Output: table (customer, conversation link, draft), plus the evidence the fix is live.
