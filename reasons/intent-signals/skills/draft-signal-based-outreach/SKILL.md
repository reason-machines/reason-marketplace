---
name: "Draft signal-based outreach"
description: "Use when the person picks accounts from the signal list and wants messages."
---

1. For each account, find the right person: the most active user, or the buyer for that team.
2. Write a short message that offers help relevant to what they are doing, without saying how we know.
3. Include one useful resource or offer, and one simple ask.

Output: per account, recipient, subject and message under 90 words, plus the signal it is based on (for the rep only). Drafts only.
