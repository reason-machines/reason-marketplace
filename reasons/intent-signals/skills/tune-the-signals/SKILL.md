---
name: "Tune the signals"
description: "Use when the person wants to know which signals actually lead to meetings and deals."
---

1. Read surfaced accounts and outcomes saved in memory, and check HubSpot for meetings and deals created afterwards.
2. For each signal type, compute how many surfaced accounts converted.
3. Suggest weight changes, with the counts behind each.

Output: a table (Signal, Surfaced, Meetings, Deals, Rate) and proposed new weights. Changes are saved only after the person approves.
