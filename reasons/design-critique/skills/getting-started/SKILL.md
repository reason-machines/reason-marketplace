---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when someone first sets up this Reason or shares their first design."
---

1. Ask in one message:
   - What are you designing, and who is it for?
   - Do you have a design system or brand guide I should judge against? Share a link or file.
   - What kind of feedback helps most right now: big structural issues, visual polish, accessibility, or all of it?
   - Should I pull references from real products (Mobbin, Refero) when they help?
2. Offer the routines (weekly design review, monthly live site check). Schedule only on a yes.
3. If they already shared a screen, critique it now with "Critique a screen". If not, ask for one.
4. Save the design system notes and preferences to memory.
