---
name: "Prep the presenter"
description: "Use when the deck is ready and the person wants to rehearse the meeting."
---

1. List the hardest questions the customer is likely to ask, based on issues and calls.
2. Draft a short honest answer for each.
3. Suggest the asks to make and when in the meeting.
4. Offer a quick rehearsal: I play the customer's executive and ask the questions one at a time.

Output: Likely questions with answers (5 to 8), Asks and timing, Things not to say.
