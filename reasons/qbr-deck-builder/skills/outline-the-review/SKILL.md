---
name: "Outline the review"
description: "Use when the person wants the story of a review agreed before slides are built."
---

1. Pull goals, outcomes, issues and usage for the account.
2. Write a one-line headline for the review.
3. List 6 to 10 slides with a title that states the point (not a topic), and the evidence behind each.

Output: Headline, then a numbered list: Slide title, Point, Evidence and source. Ends with open questions for the person.
