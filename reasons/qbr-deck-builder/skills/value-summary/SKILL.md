---
name: "Value summary"
description: "Use when the person needs a short written summary of value delivered for an email or renewal."
---

1. Gather the top three outcomes with numbers and sources.
2. Tie each to a goal the customer stated.
3. Add one sentence on what comes next, tied to their plans for the coming quarter.
4. Write under 200 words in plain language, no jargon, no inflated claims.

Output: a short summary ready to paste into an email, with a source list underneath for the person only. Nothing is sent to the customer by me.
