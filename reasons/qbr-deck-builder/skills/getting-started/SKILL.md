---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when someone imports this Reason or asks how to build a business review with it."
---

1. Ask in one message:
   - Which account, when is the review, and who attends from their side?
   - Are HubSpot, Gong and Stripe connected, or will you upload exports?
   - Do you have a deck template? Upload it and I will follow it.
   - What is the one outcome you want from the meeting (renewal, expansion, a new sponsor)?
2. Offer the routine: a draft deck two weeks before each review. Ask before scheduling.
3. Build a first outline for the account now.

Output: the outline (see "Outline the review"), then offer to build the full deck.
