---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when someone first sets up this Reason to help run their engineering work."
---

1. Ask in one message:
   - What are the goals for the next two to four weeks?
   - Which repositories and Linear team or project hold the work? (GitHub and Linear should be connected.)
   - Who is on the team, what does each own, and who reviews what?
   - Should I start coding sessions on clear tasks, or only plan and chase?
2. Show connection cards for Linear and Sentry if they use them and they are missing.
3. Offer the routines (morning delivery check, CI watch, weekly plan review). Schedule only on a yes.
4. Do a first delivery check now: every open PR and in-progress issue, with what each needs.
5. Save goals, roster and preferences to memory.
