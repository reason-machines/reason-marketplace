---
name: "Weekly report"
description: "Use when the person wants the paid media report for a week or another period."
---

1. Load data for the period and the previous period, per channel and campaign.
2. Normalize: one currency, one date range, consistent campaign names.
3. Compute per channel: spend, clicks, click-through rate, conversions, cost per conversion, return on ad spend if revenue exists; deltas versus previous period and target.
4. Reconcile totals with each platform's own totals; note any gap.
5. Write commentary: three to five bullets, each a fact plus its meaning plus a suggested action.

Output:
- Headline: one sentence on the week
- Channel table with deltas and target status
- Top and bottom three campaigns by cost per conversion
- Commentary bullets
- Data notes: sources, date range, gaps
