---
name: "Monthly deck"
description: "Use when the person needs a monthly or quarterly paid media summary for leadership."
---

1. Build the month's channel table, month over month and (when data exists) year over year.
2. Make two or three charts on my computer: spend and conversions by channel, cost per conversion trend, share of spend.
3. Write five slides worth of content: summary, channel performance, what worked, what did not, plan for next month with budget asks.
4. Produce a slide-style Markdown file or a presentation file and keep it with the conversation.

Output: the file, plus the summary text in chat. Every chart labels its source and period.
