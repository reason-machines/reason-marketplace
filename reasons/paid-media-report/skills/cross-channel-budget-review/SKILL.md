---
name: "Cross-channel budget review"
description: "Use when the person wants to know where to move budget across channels."
---

1. For each channel, compare cost per conversion and conversion volume over the last 30 and 90 days.
2. Look for diminishing returns: weeks with higher spend and worse cost per conversion.
3. Propose a reallocation (for example move 10% from channel A to B) with the expected range of outcomes, and the risks.
4. Suggest a test design to confirm: how long, what to measure.

Output: current split, proposed split, reasoning table, and the test plan. Proposals only; no campaign changes.
