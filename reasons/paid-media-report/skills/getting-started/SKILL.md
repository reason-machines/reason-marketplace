---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when the person first sets up reporting or asks what this Reason does."
---

1. Ask in one message:
   - Which channels do you run, and are Supermetrics, Windsor.ai, X Ads or TikTok for Business connected? Otherwise, upload last week's exports.
   - Which conversion counts, and what are your targets per channel?
   - Who reads the report, and how long should it be?
   - Weekly, monthly, or both?
2. Save definitions, targets and audience to memory.
3. Offer the weekly and monthly routines; ask before scheduling.
4. Build the first report now with "Weekly report" from the data available.

Output: confirmed definitions, the routine offer and the first report.
