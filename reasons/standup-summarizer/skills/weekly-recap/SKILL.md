---
name: "Weekly recap"
description: "Use when someone wants a summary of the team's week or a sprint."
---

1. Collect the week's merged PRs and closed issues for the team's scope.
2. Group them by project or theme, not by person.
3. List what carried over: open PRs and in-progress issues, with age.
4. Note anything that shipped to users, if releases or deploys are visible.

Output:
- **Finished** (grouped bullets with links)
- **Carrying over** (item, owner, age)
- **Worth discussing** (repeated blockers, large PRs stuck, scope that grew)
No per-person scoring.
