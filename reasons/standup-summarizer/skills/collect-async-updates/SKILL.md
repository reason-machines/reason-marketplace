---
name: "Collect async updates"
description: "Use when people send their own updates in chat or Slack and want them merged into one standup."
---

1. Read each update the team posted to me (in this conversation or the Slack thread I was asked in).
2. Match each to the person and to the PRs or issues it mentions; add links where I can find them.
3. Fill gaps from GitHub and the tracker, marked as "from activity" so people know it was not their own words.
4. Flag anyone who has not posted, without chasing them unless asked.

Output: the standard standup format, with each person's own words kept short and a "missing updates" line at the end.
