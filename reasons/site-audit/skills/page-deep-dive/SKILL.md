---
name: "Page deep dive"
description: "Use when the person wants one important page reviewed in depth."
---

1. Fetch and render the page; screenshot desktop and mobile widths.
2. Review search basics, content clarity, speed (largest resources, timings), accessibility and the call to action.
3. Compare with two competitor pages for the same intent.
4. Write specific fixes with suggested copy where relevant.

Output: an issue list ordered by impact, suggested title and meta description, screenshots, and the competitor comparison.
