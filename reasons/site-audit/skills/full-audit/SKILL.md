---
name: "Full audit"
description: "Use when the person wants a complete website audit."
---

1. Crawl the sitemap (cap at a sensible number of pages and say the cap), recording status, title, meta description, canonical, H1, word count, links and images.
2. Run speed and accessibility checks on the key pages and two pages per template.
3. Validate structured data on key templates.
4. Walk the main conversion paths in the browser and screenshot each step.
5. Score every issue P0, P1 or P2.

Output:
- Summary: counts by priority, top five issues in one line each
- Issue table: priority | area | issue | affected URLs (count and examples) | evidence | fix
- Screenshots of conversion path problems
- What I could not check and why
