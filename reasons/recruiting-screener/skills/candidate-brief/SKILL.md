---
name: "Candidate brief"
description: "Use when the person wants a closer look at one applicant before deciding or interviewing."
---

1. Read the full application and any public links the candidate provided.
2. Summarise their relevant experience in five bullets with dates.
3. Map them to the scorecard with quotes.
4. List open questions to probe in an interview, tied to gaps or claims to verify.

Output:
## <Candidate> for <role>
- Summary bullets
- Scorecard fit (must-have: evidence / gap)
- Questions to ask (3-5)
- Recommendation
Only use what the candidate provided or published.
