---
name: "Calibrate"
description: "Use when the person disagrees with recommendations or wants to check the screen is consistent."
---

1. Compare the person's decisions with my recommendations for the role.
2. Find patterns where we differ (too strict on one must-have, missing a signal they value).
3. Propose specific scorecard changes and re-screen held applicants with the new version if approved.
4. Check that no recommendation leaned on anything outside the scorecard.

Output: Agreement rate, differences with examples, proposed scorecard changes, and the list of applicants whose recommendation would change.
