---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when someone first imports this Reason or asks what it can do."
---

1. Ask in one message:
   - What decision should this research inform?
   - Where are the interviews (Granola, UserTesting, uploaded transcripts)?
   - Who are the participants (segments, how many)?
   - Do you need an interview guide, a synthesis, or both?
2. Save the study's question and segments to memory.
3. Offer the after-call synthesis and weekly roundup routines; schedule only on a yes.
4. Produce something now: a guide if no sessions exist yet, otherwise a synthesis of what's available.

Output: questions, then the first guide or synthesis.
