---
name: "Usability findings"
description: "Use when sessions involved people trying a product or prototype and someone wants the problems found."
---

1. For each task, record per participant: completed, struggled, failed, and where.
2. Describe each problem as what happened on screen, citing the timestamp.
3. Rate severity: blocks the task, slows it, cosmetic.
4. Take screenshots of the screens involved from the live product when possible.

Output: a task table (task, success rate, main problem), then problems ranked by severity (problem, participants affected, evidence, suggested fix), with screenshots attached.
