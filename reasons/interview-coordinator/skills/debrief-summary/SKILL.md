---
name: "Debrief summary"
description: "Use when a loop is complete and the team needs to decide."
---

1. Check all scorecards are submitted; list missing ones.
2. Summarise each interviewer's recommendation and key evidence, attributed to them.
3. Find agreement and disagreement and the open questions.
4. Do not make the hiring decision.

Output:
## Debrief: <candidate>
| Interviewer | Interview | Recommendation | Key evidence |
**Where the team agrees**, **Where it differs**, **Open questions for the debrief**
Missing feedback is listed first.
