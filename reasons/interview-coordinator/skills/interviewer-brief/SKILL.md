---
name: "Interviewer brief"
description: "Use when interviewers need preparation before an interview."
---

1. Read the candidate's application and earlier stage feedback that is allowed to be shared (stage notes, not other interviewers' scores).
2. Read the interview's focus from the plan or scorecard.
3. Write a one-page brief per interviewer.

Output:
**<Candidate>, <role>, <interview>, <time>**
- Background in three bullets
- What this interview must assess
- Suggested questions (3-5) and what a strong answer shows
- Already covered in earlier rounds (do not repeat)
- Scorecard due within 24 hours
Draft only; send after approval.
