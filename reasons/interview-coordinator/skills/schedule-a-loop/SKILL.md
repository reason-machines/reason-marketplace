---
name: "Schedule a loop"
description: "Use when a candidate needs an interview loop scheduled."
---

1. Get the loop plan for the role and the candidate's availability and time zone.
2. Read interviewers' free/busy for those windows.
3. Build a schedule: required order, buffers of at least ten minutes, a break every two hours, no interviewer over their weekly cap.
4. Propose one primary schedule and one backup.
5. Draft calendar invites and the candidate message.
6. After explicit approval, create the invites and save the status to memory.

Output: table Time (candidate tz / interviewer tz) | Interview | Interviewer | Link. Then the drafts. Nothing is sent before approval.
