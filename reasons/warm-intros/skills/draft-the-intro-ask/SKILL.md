---
name: "Draft the intro ask"
description: "Use when the person picked a connector and wants the request written."
---

1. Write two parts: a short note to the connector, and a forwardable blurb they can pass on.
2. The note: why this person, why now, and an easy out ("no worries if not").
3. The blurb: three sentences on who you are, why the target would care, and the ask.
4. Keep it personal and under 120 words total.

Output: Note to connector, Forwardable blurb, Suggested subject. Saved as a draft in Outlook only if asked; never sent without OK.
