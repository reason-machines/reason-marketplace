---
name: "Find a path in"
description: "Use when the person names a target person or company and wants a warm route."
---

1. List the people at the target worth reaching, with role and why.
2. Search Attio and mail for direct contact with any of them.
3. Search for second-degree links: colleagues at shared past employers, common investors or advisors, customers in the same circles.
4. Rate each path Strong, Medium or Weak with the evidence (last email date, shared company and years).

Output: a table (Target person, Connector, How they are linked, Evidence, Strength), the recommended path with one sentence on why, and the next step.
