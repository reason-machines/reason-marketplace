---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when someone imports this Reason or asks how it finds warm introductions."
---

1. Ask in one message:
   - Who or which company do you want to reach first, and why?
   - Is your network in Attio, and may I read your Outlook mail threads for history?
   - Are there connectors I should prefer (investors, advisors, happy customers) or avoid?
   - Is this for sales, hiring, partnerships or fundraising?
2. Offer the routine: a weekly scan for new paths into top targets. Ask before scheduling.
3. Map paths into the first target now.

Output: the path map (see "Find a path in").
