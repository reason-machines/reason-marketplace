---
name: "Relationship map"
description: "Use when the person wants to see who they know across a list of accounts."
---

1. Take the account list.
2. For each, find your direct contacts and best second-degree path.
3. Mark accounts with no path at all.
4. Note connectors who appear for several accounts; one conversation with them may open many doors.

Output: a table (Account, Best contact or connector, Strength, Last interaction), sorted strongest first, a list of multi-account connectors, and a list of accounts that need a cold approach.
