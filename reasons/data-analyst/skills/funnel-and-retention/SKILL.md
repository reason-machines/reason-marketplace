---
name: "Funnel and retention"
description: "Use when someone wants a conversion funnel, activation rate or cohort retention analysis."
---

1. Agree the steps (for a funnel) or the cohort event and return event (for retention), and the conversion window.
2. Build it in the connected analytics tool if possible; otherwise compute from an event export in Python.
3. Split by the one or two segments the person cares about.
4. Mark the biggest drop-off and the cohort that retains best.
5. Note sample sizes per step and per cohort.

Output: a funnel table (step, users, step conversion, overall) or a retention triangle (cohort by week), a chart, and three bullet takeaways with numbers.

Never compare cohorts of very different sizes without saying so.
