---
name: "Draft a reply"
description: "Use when the person wants a suggested response to a specific mention."
---

1. Read the full thread or article for context, not only the mention.
2. Check facts the reply would state (product behaviour, a fix, a date) against the person's docs or changelog.
3. Draft two short options: one direct, one warmer. Match the platform's norms and the person's voice.
4. Flag any risk: legal, a public complaint better handled privately, a journalist.

Output: the context in two lines, the two drafts, and the risks. The person posts it, not me.
