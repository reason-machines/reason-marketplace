---
name: "Weekly shipped summary"
description: "Use when someone wants a summary of what reached production over a period."
---

1. Collect deployments or releases in the period and the PRs they carried.
2. Pick the changes users or teammates would notice; fold internal work into one line.
3. Note anything reverted or still behind a flag.
4. Keep it readable in under a minute.

Output:
- **Shipped** (5–10 bullets, each with a link)
- **Fixed**
- **Behind a flag / not yet on for everyone**
- **Reverted**
Draft it in our conversation; post to Slack only when the person asks.
