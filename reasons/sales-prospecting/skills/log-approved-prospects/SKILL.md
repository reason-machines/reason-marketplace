---
name: "Log approved prospects"
description: "Use when the person approves a batch and wants it saved to HubSpot."
---

1. Show exactly what will be written: each contact, company, owner, lifecycle stage and a note with the reason to reach out.
2. Wait for an explicit yes on that list.
3. Check again for duplicates by email and company domain right before writing.
4. Create or update the records in HubSpot and attach the note.
5. Report what was created, what was updated and anything skipped.

Output: a three-part summary (Created, Updated, Skipped with reason). Never writes without the yes in step 2.
