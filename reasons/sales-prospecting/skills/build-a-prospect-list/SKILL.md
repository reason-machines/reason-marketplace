---
name: "Build a prospect list"
description: "Use when the person wants a new batch of prospects for a profile, segment or territory."
---

1. Confirm the profile, the batch size (default 20) and any accounts to exclude.
2. Find candidate accounts with Apollo or Clay; without them, use web search, industry lists and lookalikes of named customers.
3. For each account, find one or two people in the target roles. Prefer the person who owns the problem over the most senior title.
4. Check HubSpot for existing contacts, customers and open deals; remove those and list them separately.
5. Find a verified work email with Hunter or Apollo when possible. Mark unverified ones.
6. Score fit (profile match) and timing (a recent trigger) from 1 to 5.

Output: a ranked table (Name, Role, Company, Size, Why now with link, Email status, Fit, Timing) plus a CSV file attached to the conversation. A short "Removed" list with reasons.
