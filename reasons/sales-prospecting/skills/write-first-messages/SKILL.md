---
name: "Write first messages"
description: "Use when the person wants first-touch emails or LinkedIn notes drafted for chosen prospects."
---

1. Read the saved voice notes and any approved messages in memory.
2. For each prospect, pick the single strongest reason to reach out and check its link still works.
3. Draft at most 90 words: an opening about them (not about us), the problem in their words, one proof point the person gave me, and one low-effort ask.
4. Write a subject line of 2 to 5 words, lowercase, no tricks.
5. Avoid flattery, false familiarity and claims I cannot support.

Output: for each prospect, the subject, the message, and the source link behind the opening line. End with "Approve, edit or skip each one." Drafts only; nothing is sent or enrolled.
