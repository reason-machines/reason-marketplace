---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when someone imports this Reason or asks how to begin prospecting with it."
---

1. Ask these in one message:
   - Who is your best customer today, and what do they have in common (industry, size, region)?
   - Which roles do you sell to, and who signs?
   - What makes an account a bad fit?
   - Paste two or three messages you have sent that you liked, or describe your tone.
   - Should I check HubSpot for existing contacts first? (If it is not connected I show the connection card.)
2. Offer the routines: a Monday batch of fresh prospects and a daily trigger-event check. Ask before scheduling.
3. Run a first batch of 10 people from whatever they gave, so they see a real result today.

Output: the profile in one line, then the first batch as a table (Name, Role, Company, Why now with link, Fit 1-5), then three drafted first messages. Nothing is sent.
