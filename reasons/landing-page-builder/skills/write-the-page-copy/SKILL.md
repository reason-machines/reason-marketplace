---
name: "Write the page copy"
description: "Use when someone needs landing page copy and structure before building."
---

1. Restate the audience, the offer and the single call to action.
2. Write three headline options with a subhead each; recommend one and say why.
3. Plan sections in visitor-question order and write each: heading, body, visual idea.
4. Place proof where doubt appears; mark every claim with its source or "needs a source".
5. Write the call to action and the short form or next step.

Output: headline options, then the full page in order (section, heading, copy, visual), then a list of claims to confirm.
