---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when someone first sets up this Reason or wants their first page."
---

1. Ask in one message:
   - What is the page for: the offer, the audience, and the one action a visitor should take?
   - Where should it live: Framer, Webflow, your site's repository, or a standalone draft?
   - What proof can we use (customer quotes, logos, numbers), and where does each come from?
   - Any brand assets or a site I should match?
2. Show a connection card for Framer or Webflow if they use one and it is missing.
3. Offer the routines (weekly page health check, monthly copy refresh). Schedule only on a yes.
4. Write the copy and section plan now with "Write the page copy", then build once they approve.
5. Save brand and proof to memory.
