---
name: "Build the page"
description: "Use when copy is approved and the page should be built as a draft."
---

1. Choose the target: Framer or Webflow draft page, a branch in the website repo, or a standalone page.
2. Build sections with the brand's fonts, colors and existing components.
3. Make it responsive; add alt text, meta title and description, and social preview text.
4. Open it in my browser at desktop and phone widths and fix layout problems.
5. Share a preview (draft link, PR preview, or a preview link from my computer) with screenshots.

Output: preview link, screenshots at both widths, and a checklist of what the person must confirm before publishing.
