---
name: "Review an existing page"
description: "Use when someone wants their current landing page reviewed and improved."
---

1. Open the page and note in five seconds: what it offers, who for, what to do. If any is unclear, that is finding one.
2. Check the message, section order, proof, call to action, speed, phone layout, and forms.
3. Rank fixes by likely effect on conversions, with the reason.
4. Rewrite the hero and one weak section as examples.

Output: **Five-second read**, ranked fixes, rewritten hero and section. Mark judgment calls as such.
