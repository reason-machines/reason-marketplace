---
name: "Deal deep dive"
description: "Use when the person asks about one specific deal's status and risk."
---

1. Pull the deal, its contacts, activities and notes.
2. Build a timeline of touches and stage changes.
3. Assess: who is engaged, what is the next step, what could stop it.
4. Compare it with similar deals that were won, if the CRM has them.
5. Suggest two actions to move it forward, each with an owner.

Output: Timeline (dated bullets), Who is involved, Risks, Next two actions. No field is changed without approval.
