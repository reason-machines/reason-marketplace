---
name: "Forecast check"
description: "Use when the person needs to call a number for the month or quarter."
---

1. Take deals with close dates in the period.
2. Group by forecast category and owner.
3. For each commit deal, check: recent activity, a dated next step, a known decision maker, paperwork status. Mark weak evidence.
4. Compute a conservative, likely and stretch number, explaining what each includes.

Output: a table (Category, Count, Amount), the three numbers with their reasoning, and a list of commit deals with weak evidence.
