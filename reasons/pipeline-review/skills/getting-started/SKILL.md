---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when someone imports this Reason or asks how to set up pipeline reviews."
---

1. Ask in one message:
   - Is your pipeline in HubSpot or Attio, or will you upload an export?
   - Which pipeline and team should I review, and what is this period's target?
   - How do you define your stages and forecast categories?
   - What counts as stale for you (default: no activity in 14 days)?
2. Offer the routines: a Monday brief and a Thursday no-next-step nudge. Ask before scheduling.
3. Take the first snapshot and produce the first brief now. Note that "what changed" starts next week.

Output: the weekly pipeline brief (see "Weekly pipeline brief").
