---
name: "Hygiene pass"
description: "Use when the person wants the CRM cleaned up before a review."
---

1. Find deals with missing amount, close date, owner, next step, or a close date in the past.
2. Group by owner.
3. Draft the exact field change suggested for each where the right value is clear from activity; otherwise ask.
4. Wait for approval, then apply only the approved changes.

Output: per owner, a table (Deal, Problem, Suggested fix). After approval: a list of what was changed.
