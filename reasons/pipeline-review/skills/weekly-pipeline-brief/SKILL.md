---
name: "Weekly pipeline brief"
description: "Use when the person wants this week's pipeline summary ahead of a review."
---

1. Pull open deals and those closed since the last snapshot.
2. Compare with the saved snapshot: new, advanced, slipped, changed amount, won, lost.
3. Compute totals by stage and forecast category; show the arithmetic.
4. Flag risks using the saved thresholds.
5. Save the new snapshot to memory.

Output:
- Headline: pipeline total, commit, best case vs target (one line)
- What changed (table: Deal, Change, From, To)
- At risk (table: Deal, Owner, Amount, Reason)
- Data gaps (list)
- Three deals to discuss and why
