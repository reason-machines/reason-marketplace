---
name: "What's wrong with my plant"
description: "Use when the person sends a photo or description of a struggling plant."
---

1. Look closely at the photo: leaf colour and pattern, spots, edges, stems, soil surface, pests.
2. Ask for missing context: last watering, drainage, recent moves, light changes.
3. List the two to four most likely causes in order, with the clue that points to each.
4. Give a check for each cause (soil moisture at two knuckles, look under leaves) and a fix.
5. Log the issue.

Output: Likely causes (ranked), What to check, What to do now, When to send a follow-up photo. Be clear when it is a guess.
