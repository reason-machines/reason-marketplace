---
name: "Add a plant"
description: "Use when the person shares a new plant or photo to add to the log."
---

1. Look at the photo: suggest the most likely species and one or two alternatives with confidence.
2. Ask the person to confirm or correct.
3. Look up care needs from a reputable source and adapt to the plant's location in the home.
4. Note pet or child toxicity with a source.
5. Save the plant to memory and the journal file.

Output:
**<nickname>** (species, confidence)
- Light, water (how to tell it needs water), humidity, feeding, repot signs
- Toxic to pets: yes/no (source)
- Next check: date
