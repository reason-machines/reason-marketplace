---
name: "Weekly plant round"
description: "Use when the weekly routine runs or the person asks what needs care today."
---

1. Read the log: last watering, feeding and issues for each plant, plus the season.
2. List plants to check, with what to look for (soil dry two centimetres down, new growth, pests).
3. Mention any feeding due and any plant with an open issue to re-photograph.
4. Ask the person to reply with what they did, and log it.

Output: a short checklist by room: [ ] plant - check / do. End with "Reply with what you watered and I'll log it."
