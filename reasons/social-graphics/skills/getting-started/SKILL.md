---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when someone first sets up this Reason or asks for their first graphic."
---

1. Ask in one message:
   - Which channels do you post on, and which sizes do you need?
   - Is Canva connected, and do you have a brand kit or templates there?
   - Any brand rules I must follow (colors, fonts, logo placement, things to avoid)?
   - What is the first thing you need a graphic for?
2. Show a Canva connection card if they use Canva and it is not connected.
3. Offer the routines (weekly graphics batch, event countdown set). Schedule only on a yes.
4. Make the first graphic now with "Make a graphic set".
5. Save brand rules and sizes to memory.
