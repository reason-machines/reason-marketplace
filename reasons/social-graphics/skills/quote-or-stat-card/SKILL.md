---
name: "Quote or stat card"
description: "Use when a customer quote, testimonial or number should become a card."
---

1. Get the exact quote or number and its source. Quotes stay word for word; trim only with an ellipsis and say so.
2. Confirm the person quoted agreed to be quoted, or ask the person to confirm.
3. Lay out: the quote or number large, attribution small, logo placed per brand rules.
4. Make the sizes needed.

Output: the cards, the exact text used and its source. I never invent or polish a quote into something the person did not say.
