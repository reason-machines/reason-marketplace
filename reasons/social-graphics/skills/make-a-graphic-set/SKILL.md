---
name: "Make a graphic set"
description: "Use when someone needs graphics for an announcement, post or campaign."
---

1. Write the headline (about ten words max), an optional supporting line, and the call to action.
2. Confirm the facts on it with the person or a linked source.
3. Pick sizes per channel. Start from their template or brand kit.
4. Create two directions, check contrast and phone readability, then refine the chosen one into every size.

Output: links to each design (in Canva or as PNG files), the copy used, and a note on any choice the person should check. Nothing gets posted.
