---
name: "Carousel"
description: "Use when a post should be a multi-slide carousel, such as a how-to or a list."
---

1. Outline the slides: a hook slide, one idea per slide, and a closing slide with the call to action. Usually five to eight slides.
2. Write the copy for each slide and get approval before designing.
3. Design with a consistent layout so the slides read as one set, and number them.
4. Export each slide in the platform's size.

Output: the outline, the slide copy, and the design links or PNG files in order.
