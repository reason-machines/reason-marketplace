---
name: "Quick answer"
description: "Use when the person needs a fast, sourced answer rather than a full report."
---

1. Search for the most authoritative current source on the question.
2. Read it and one independent confirming source.
3. Answer in a short paragraph with both links.
4. If the sources conflict or the answer is time-sensitive, say so in one line and offer a deep report.

Output: Answer paragraph, then "Sources:" with two links and dates, then "Confidence:" one word. Keep it under 150 words. Do not pad with background the person did not ask for.
