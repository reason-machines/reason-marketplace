---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when the person first sets up this Reason or asks how it works."
---

1. Ask in one message:
   - What question or decision are you working on right now?
   - How deep should I go: a quick answer, a one-page brief, or a full report?
   - Any sources you trust most, or ones I should avoid?
   - Who is the report for (you, your team, a client)?
   - Are there topics you want me to watch over time?
2. Save preferences to memory.
3. Offer the routines (topic watch, source check, reading queue digest) and create only the ones accepted.
4. If they gave a question, start the research immediately using the Deep report skill at the depth they chose. If not, offer three example questions based on what they said.

Output: the questions, then the first result. Never schedule without a yes.
