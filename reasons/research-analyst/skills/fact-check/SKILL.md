---
name: "Fact-check"
description: "Use when the person shares a claim, article, post or draft and wants to know what holds up."
---

1. Extract every checkable claim (numbers, dates, quotes, causal statements) into a numbered list.
2. For each, find the original source: the dataset, paper, filing or full quote in context.
3. Rate each claim: Supported, Partly supported (explain what is off), Not supported, or Could not verify.
4. Note misleading framing even when the facts are technically right.

Output: a table with columns Claim | Verdict | What the source says | Link. Then a two-sentence overall assessment. Never mark a claim Not supported just because you could not find it; use Could not verify.
