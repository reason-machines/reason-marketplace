---
name: "Topic update"
description: "Use when the topic watch routine runs or the person asks what changed on a topic."
---

1. Read the last report or note on this topic from memory.
2. Search for news, publications and data released since that date.
3. Keep only items that change, strengthen or weaken an earlier conclusion, or are genuinely new.
4. Update the saved conclusion in memory.

Output:
## What changed since <date>
- item, why it matters, link
## Does the conclusion still hold? (yes / partly / no, one paragraph)
If nothing meaningful changed, say so in one line rather than listing filler.
