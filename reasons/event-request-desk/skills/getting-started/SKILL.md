---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when someone first imports this Reason or asks what it can do."
---

1. Ask in one message:
   - What are you trying to get from events (pipeline, hiring, brand, community)?
   - Who's your audience, and which regions matter?
   - What's your events and sponsorship budget for this quarter, and what's committed?
   - Who can speak, and how many trips a month are realistic?
   - Paste a decline you've sent before, so I can match your tone.
2. Save criteria, budget and tone to memory.
3. Offer the weekly roundup, deadline watch and quarterly review; schedule only on a yes.
4. Score whatever requests they paste now with "Score a request".

Output: questions, then the first scored requests with drafts.
