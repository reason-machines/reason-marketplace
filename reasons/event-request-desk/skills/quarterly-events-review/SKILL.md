---
name: "Quarterly events review"
description: "Use when someone wants to know what events cost and returned."
---

1. List events done this quarter with cost and effort.
2. Gather outcomes the person can share (leads, hires, meetings) and label anything estimated.
3. Compute cost per outcome where data exists.
4. Recommend what to repeat, change or stop.

Output: table (event, cost, outcomes, cost per outcome, verdict), remaining budget, and three recommendations.
