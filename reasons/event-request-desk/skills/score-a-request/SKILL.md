---
name: "Score a request"
description: "Use when an event, sponsorship or speaking invitation needs a decision."
---

1. Extract: organizer, event, ask type, dates, location, cost, deadline.
2. Research with links: past editions, audience, sponsors, speakers, reviews.
3. Score each criterion 1–5 with one line of evidence; apply the saved weights.
4. Compare cost with remaining budget.
5. Recommend yes / maybe / no.

Output: a card (event, ask, cost, deadline), the score table, recommendation in one line, and the draft reply. Never send it.
