---
name: "Weekly family plan"
description: "Use when the Sunday routine runs or the person asks what the week looks like."
---

1. Read next week's calendar and open tasks.
2. Lay out each day: who goes where, when, and who is driving.
3. Find conflicts and uncovered pickups.
4. List deadlines, payments and items to bring, by day.
5. Build a short shopping list from notices and tasks.

Output:
## Week of <date>
**Mon** - kid: activity time, driver; due: item
... each day
## Conflicts to solve
## Due this week
## To buy
Keep it phone-readable.
