---
name: "Notice to tasks"
description: "Use when the person shares a school email, flyer, newsletter or schedule."
---

1. Read the whole notice, including attachments and images.
2. Extract each actionable item: date and time, what, for whom, cost, what to bring, form or reply needed, deadline.
3. Quote the source line for anything ambiguous.
4. Propose calendar events (with reminders) and tasks.

Output: a table: For | What | When | Action needed | Deadline. Then "Add these?" and wait. After yes, create the events and tasks and list what was created. Never pay, sign or reply.
