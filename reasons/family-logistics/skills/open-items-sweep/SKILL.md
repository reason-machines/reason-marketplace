---
name: "Open items sweep"
description: "Use when the deadline sweep runs or the person asks what is still outstanding."
---

1. Read the open items list from memory and Todoist.
2. Sort by deadline; mark anything overdue or due within three days.
3. For each, say exactly what is needed to close it (sign the form, pay the fee on the school portal, reply yes or no).
4. Remove items the person says are done.

Output: Overdue, Due in 3 days, Later, each as a checklist line with what to do. Never mark something done unless the person says so.
