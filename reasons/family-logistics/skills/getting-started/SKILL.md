---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when the person first sets up family logistics."
---

1. Ask in one message:
   - Who is in the family, and which school, grade or activities does each child have?
   - What are the regular weekly commitments (pickups, practices, lessons) and who usually covers them?
   - Which calendar should I use, and should I add events there or just list them?
   - Do you want tasks in Todoist?
   - When should the weekly plan arrive?
2. Check Microsoft 365 Calendar and Todoist; offer connection cards if missing.
3. Save the family profile to memory.
4. Offer the routines (Sunday plan, morning heads-up, deadline sweep); create only what is accepted.
5. Ask them to paste the latest school email or schedule and process it with Notice to tasks.

Output: the questions, then the first processed notice or a first weekly plan.
