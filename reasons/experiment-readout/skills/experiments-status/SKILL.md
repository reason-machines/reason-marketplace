name: "Experiments status"
description: "Use when the weekly check runs or someone asks what's running."
---

1. List every running experiment with start date, planned end, and current exposures.
2. For each, say whether it has reached its sample size, and flag health problems.
3. Note experiments past their end date that still need a decision, and flags fully rolled out that could be cleaned up.

Output: table (experiment, days running, progress to sample, health, action needed), then a short list of decisions waiting on someone.
