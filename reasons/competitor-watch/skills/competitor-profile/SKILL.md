---
name: "Competitor profile"
description: "Use when the person wants a full picture of one competitor."
---

1. Read the homepage, pricing, product pages, docs, changelog, careers and recent blog posts.
2. Search news and social for launches, funding and leadership changes from the last six months.
3. Read recent public reviews for recurring praise and complaints (quote briefly, link each).
4. Pull traffic trend from Similarweb if connected.

Output (one page):
- Positioning in their words
- Target customer
- Pricing and packaging table
- Recent launches (dated)
- Hiring signals: open roles by team
- Strengths and weaknesses with evidence
- Where we win and lose against them
