---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when the person first sets up competitor tracking."
---

1. Ask in one message:
   - Which competitors (names or URLs)? Three to eight is a good start.
   - Which pages matter most: pricing, homepage, changelog, careers, docs, blog?
   - What is your own product and pricing, briefly, so I can say why a change matters?
   - How often do you want to hear, and how much detail?
2. Find each competitor's tracked pages and confirm the list.
3. Take baseline snapshots now (text and pricing screenshots) and save them with the conversation.
4. Offer the weekly digest and daily pricing check; ask before scheduling.
5. Deliver a first one-page profile per competitor.

Output: tracked page list, the routine offer, and the first profiles.
