---
name: "Messaging shift"
description: "Use when the person wants to know how a competitor's positioning or messaging has changed over time."
---

1. Collect the competitor's homepage headline, subhead and main sections from the saved snapshots, plus archived versions from public web archives when available.
2. Line them up by date.
3. Identify themes gained and dropped (audience, use case, claims, proof points).
4. Compare with the person's own homepage messaging.

Output: a dated timeline of headlines, a themes gained and dropped list, and three implications for our messaging. Quote headlines exactly with source links.
