---
name: "Pricing comparison"
description: "Use when the person wants their pricing compared side by side with competitors."
---

1. Capture each competitor's current plans: name, price (monthly and annual), unit (seat, usage, flat), limits and key included features.
2. Normalize to a common unit where possible (for example price for a 10-person team) and show the math.
3. Add the person's own plans.
4. Note where a comparison is not like for like.

Output: a comparison table, a normalized cost table for two or three typical customer sizes, and five observations. Every price links to the page and capture date.
