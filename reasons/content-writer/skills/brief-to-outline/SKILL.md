---
name: "Brief to outline"
description: "Use when the person wants an outline or structure approved before a full draft is written."
---

1. Identify the reader, their question and the one takeaway.
2. Look at the top five pages currently ranking or cited for the topic (web search) and note what they all cover and what they all miss.
3. Draft an outline that covers the basics briefly and spends most of its length on what others miss: original data, a worked example, a stronger opinion or a real product walkthrough.
4. For each section, list the evidence or example it needs and whether we have it.

Output:
- Working headline and angle in one sentence
- Outline table: section | what it proves | evidence needed | have it? (yes/no)
- Gaps: what the person must supply (a customer example, a number, a screenshot)

Do not start the full draft until the person approves or edits the outline.
