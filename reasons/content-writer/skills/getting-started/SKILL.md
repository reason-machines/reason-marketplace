---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when the person first sets up this Reason or asks what it can do."
---

1. Ask these in one message:
   - Who is the main reader, and what do they already know?
   - Can you share two or three posts you are proud of (links or files) so I can learn your voice?
   - Where should drafts go: here, Notion, WordPress.com or Webflow?
   - Any words, claims or competitors I should never mention?
   - What should I write first? A topic, a brief or notes are all fine.
2. Read the sample posts and write a short voice note (tone, sentence length, favourite moves, words to avoid). Show it and save it to memory once confirmed.
3. Offer the recurring jobs (weekly ideas, monthly refresh list, draft-day check) and ask whether to schedule any. Do not create a routine without a yes.
4. Write the first piece now with the "Draft a post" playbook. If no topic was given, suggest three from their site and draft the one they pick.

Output: the voice note, the routine offer, then the first outline or draft.
