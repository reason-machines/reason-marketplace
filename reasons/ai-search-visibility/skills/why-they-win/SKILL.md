---
name: "Why they win"
description: "Use when a competitor or third-party page keeps getting cited instead of the person's own pages."
---

1. Take the questions where a competitor or a third-party page wins.
2. Open each cited page and note: page type (comparison, list, docs, review site, forum), how directly it answers the question, structure (tables, definitions, FAQ), freshness, and whether it names the person's brand.
3. Open the person's closest page for the same question and compare.
4. Write the gap in one sentence per question.

Output:
- Table: question | winning page | why it wins | our closest page | gap
- Patterns across the set (for example: competitors publish comparison tables; we have none)

Quote only short fragments and link every page.
