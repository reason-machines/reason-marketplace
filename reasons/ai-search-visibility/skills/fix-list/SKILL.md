---
name: "Fix list"
description: "Use when the person wants a prioritized plan to improve how AI answers describe and recommend them."
---

1. Start from the latest check and the "Why they win" gaps.
2. Propose fixes in three groups: pages to create, pages to restructure (clear answer first, comparison tables, dated facts, FAQ), and third-party listings or profiles to correct.
3. Score each fix by expected reach (how many tracked questions it touches) and effort (hours, rough).
4. For the top three, write a short brief: target questions, page outline, facts to state plainly, sources to cite.

Output:
- Ranked fix table: fix | questions touched | effort | why
- Three briefs ready for a writer

No fake reviews, paid placements disguised as editorial, or hidden text.
