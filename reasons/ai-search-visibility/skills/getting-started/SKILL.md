---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when the person first sets up this Reason or asks how AI search visibility works here."
---

1. Ask in one message:
   - Your brand, product names and domain?
   - Which three to five competitors should I track?
   - Which buyer questions matter most? Paste any you have; otherwise I will draft them.
   - Is Profound or Peec AI connected or available to you?
   - Which markets or languages matter?
2. If no questions were given, draft 20 from the person's site, pricing page and competitors, grouped by funnel stage, and ask them to cut or add.
3. Save the brands, competitors and question set to memory.
4. Offer the weekly check and monthly deep dive; ask before creating any routine.
5. Run the "Visibility check" playbook now on the first ten questions and report the baseline.

Output: confirmed setup, the routine offer, then the first baseline table.
