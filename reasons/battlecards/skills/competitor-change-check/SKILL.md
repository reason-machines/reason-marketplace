---
name: "Competitor change check"
description: "Use when the person wants to know what a competitor changed recently."
---

1. Visit their pricing, product, changelog and blog pages and compare with the saved snapshot.
2. Search news and posts from the last two weeks.
3. Note each real change with a date and link, and whether it affects the card.

Output: a list per competitor (Change, Date, Link, Card impact), or "No change" in one line. Offers to update affected cards.
