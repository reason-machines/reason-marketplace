---
name: "Live deal help"
description: "Use when a rep is up against a competitor in a specific deal and needs help now."
---

1. Read the deal context the rep gives (or the call transcript).
2. Pick the parts of the card that matter for this buyer's priorities.
3. Draft a short reply or talk track for the exact objection raised.
4. Keep it honest: if the competitor is genuinely stronger on this point, say how to reframe instead of deny.

Output: Talk track (under 100 words), two questions to ask, one proof point with source.
