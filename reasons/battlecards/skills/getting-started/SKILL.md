---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when someone imports this Reason or asks how battlecards are built."
---

1. Ask in one message:
   - Which competitors come up most? List up to five.
   - What do you believe your real advantages are against each?
   - Are calls in Gong, or will you paste win and loss notes?
   - Who uses the cards (reps, sales engineers, partners)?
2. Offer the routines: a weekly competitor change check and a monthly card refresh. Ask before scheduling.
3. Build the first card for the top competitor now.

Output: one battlecard (see "Build a battlecard").
