---
name: "Build a battlecard"
description: "Use when the person wants a new or rebuilt card for one competitor."
---

1. Research the competitor: positioning line, target customer, pricing and packaging, recent launches.
2. Search calls for mentions; extract why prospects like or dislike them.
3. Write the card.

Output, one page:
- In one line: how they pitch themselves
- Where they win (with evidence)
- Where we win (with evidence)
- Objections and responses (3 to 5)
- Questions to ask the prospect (3)
- Proof points to use
- Sources (links and call references with dates)
