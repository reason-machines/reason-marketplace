---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when the person first sets up their briefing."
---

1. Ask in one message:
   - Which topics, industries and companies should the briefing cover?
   - Any people or X accounts whose posts matter?
   - Sources you trust most, and ones to skip?
   - What time and time zone should it arrive, and where (here or Slack)?
   - Short (5 items) or fuller (up to 12)?
2. Check X, Readwise and You.com connections; offer connection cards for the useful ones, but work without them.
3. Save the setup to memory.
4. Offer the routines (morning briefing, weekly roundup, breaking watch). Create only what is accepted.
5. Produce today's briefing immediately with the Daily briefing skill.

Output: the questions, then the first briefing. Never schedule without a yes.
