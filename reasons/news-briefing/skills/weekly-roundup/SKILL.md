---
name: "Weekly roundup"
description: "Use when the weekly routine runs or the person wants the week in review."
---

1. Read the week's briefings from the conversation and memory.
2. Group items into themes and pick the five developments that matter most.
3. For each, explain how the story moved during the week and what to watch next.
4. Check for anything big the daily briefings missed.

Output:
# Week of <date>
1-5: theme title, three to four sentences, links
## What to watch next week (bullets)
Keep it under 500 words.
