---
name: "Daily briefing"
description: "Use when the morning routine runs or the person asks for today's news."
---

1. Load topics and the already-covered list from memory.
2. Search each topic for the last 24 hours; read the top results; collect candidates.
3. Merge duplicates; keep the original reporting source.
4. Drop anything already covered unless there is a real update.
5. Rank by relevance to the person and pick the top items.
6. Add the chosen stories to the covered list in memory.

Output:
# Briefing, <date>
**Top story**: headline, two sentences, why it matters, link
Then each item: **Headline** - what happened. Why it matters to you. (Source, link)
End with "Also noted:" one-liners, max five.
Never include an item without a link.
