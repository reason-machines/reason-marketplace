---
name: "Tune my topics"
description: "Use when the person wants to add, remove or adjust what the briefing covers."
---

1. Show the current topics, people and sources from memory as a short list.
2. Apply the person's changes; for vague additions ("more AI policy"), propose specific search terms and sources and confirm.
3. If they say an item was not useful, record why so ranking improves.
4. Save the new setup to memory.

Output: the updated list and one example headline that would now make the cut. No other changes.
