---
name: "Make it sound human"
description: "Use when the person says a text sounds robotic, generic or machine-written."
---

1. Mark the tells: stock openers and closers, stacked adjectives, lists of three used by habit, vague abstractions, uniform sentence length, hedging, overuse of colons and dashes, words like "leverage", "seamless", "unlock", "elevate".
2. Replace each with something specific: an example, a number already in the text, a plain verb.
3. Vary sentence length; allow a short sentence.
4. Keep the person's voice notes in mind.

Output: the rewritten text, then a before and after list of the five biggest changes. Do not add facts to make it more specific; ask for them instead.
