---
name: "Shorten"
description: "Use when the person needs a text cut to a length or a format (subject line, tweet, summary, abstract)."
---

1. Confirm the target: character or word count, or format.
2. Find the one point that must survive and the one supporting detail most worth keeping.
3. Cut in order: repetition, qualifiers, background the reader already knows, examples beyond the best one.
4. Give three versions when the target is short (headlines, subject lines, posts).

Output: the shortened text with its count, or three options with counts. Note anything important that had to go.
