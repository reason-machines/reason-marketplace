---
name: "Edit a draft"
description: "Use when the person pastes text and wants it edited."
---

1. Identify reader, purpose and desired length. Ask only if the purpose is unclear.
2. Structural pass: lead with the point; reorder or cut sections; note the reasons.
3. Line pass: tighten sentences, replace vague words with specific ones, remove filler and stock phrases, vary rhythm.
4. Consistency pass: names, numbers, style rules.
5. Re-read the result aloud in my head against the original meaning.

Output:
- Edited text
- Change list: grouped (structure, clarity, cuts, style) with one line of reason each
- Flags: claims that need a source or a decision from the person

Never add new claims.
