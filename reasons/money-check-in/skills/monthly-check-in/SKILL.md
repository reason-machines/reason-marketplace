name: "Monthly check-in"
description: "Use when the person uploads statements or the monthly routine runs."
---

1. Parse every file into one table with date, merchant, amount, account. Remove transfers between own accounts and pair refunds.
2. Categorise with the saved rules; list uncertain ones.
3. Total by category; compare with budget and last month.
4. Chart category totals and save the chart and CSV with the conversation.
5. Update monthly totals in memory.

Output:
## <Month> at a glance: total spent, vs budget, vs last month
| Category | This month | Budget | Last month |
## Notable: biggest changes, unusual charges
## Actions: one or two concrete steps
## Please check: uncertain categorisations
