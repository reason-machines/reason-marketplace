---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when the person first sets up their money check-in."
---

1. Ask in one message:
   - Which accounts and cards do you want to track (names only)?
   - Do you have a monthly budget or categories you already use?
   - What goal matters most right now: spend less, save a set amount, pay down a card?
   - Do you optimise card rewards for points, cash back, or not at all?
2. Ask them to upload last month's statements as CSV or PDF.
3. Save cards, goals and categories to memory.
4. Offer the monthly reminder and quarterly subscription review routines; create only what is accepted.
5. Run Monthly check-in on whatever they upload.

Output: the questions, then the first check-in. Remind them not to share full card numbers.
