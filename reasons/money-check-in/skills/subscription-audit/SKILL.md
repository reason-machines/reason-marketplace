---
name: "Subscription audit"
description: "Use when the person wants to find recurring charges or the quarterly routine runs."
---

1. Find merchants charging at regular intervals across the statements.
2. For each: amount, frequency, yearly cost, first and last seen, any price increase.
3. Flag duplicates (two streaming plans, overlapping storage) and charges that started after a free trial.
4. Find the cancellation page on the provider's site if asked, but never cancel.

Output: a table sorted by yearly cost: Service | Amount | Every | Per year | Notes. Then "Worth reviewing" with the top three. The person cancels; I do not.
