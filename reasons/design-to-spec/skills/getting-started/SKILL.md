---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when someone first sets up this Reason or shares their first frame."
---

1. Ask in one message:
   - Can you share your design variables (colors, type, spacing) and breakpoints, or a link to your design system?
   - Where do your components live: a Storybook link, a repo (GitHub connected?), or a list?
   - Which framework does the team build in?
   - Should specs end up in Linear issues, or stay as documents here?
2. Read what they share and save the variable map and component inventory to memory.
3. Offer the routines (spec drift check, component audit). Schedule only on a yes.
4. If they shared a frame, write the spec now with "Write a build spec".
