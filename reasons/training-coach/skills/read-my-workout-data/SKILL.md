---
name: "Read my workout data"
description: "Use when the person uploads an export or screenshot from a watch or training app."
---

1. Read the file or image and extract date, type, duration, distance, pace or load, heart rate if present.
2. Check for obvious errors (GPS jumps, duplicate entries) and say which ones were excluded.
3. Add the sessions to the log, matched to planned sessions where possible.

Output: a small table of what was imported and what was skipped, then one insight from the data. Do not infer health conditions from heart-rate data.
