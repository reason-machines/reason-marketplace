---
name: "Weekly plan"
description: "Use when the weekly routine runs or the person asks for next week's training."
---

1. Read the last weeks of the log and the goal.
2. Decide the week's focus and total load, increasing gradually from what was actually done (not what was planned).
3. Place hard sessions apart, with easy or rest days between, on the days the person has.
4. Write each session with purpose, warm-up, main set, cool-down, and target effort.

Output:
## Week of <date>: focus
| Day | Session | Duration | Effort |
Then each session's details. End with one line on why the week looks like this.
