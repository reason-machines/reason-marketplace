---
name: "Progress review"
description: "Use when the monthly routine runs or the person asks how they are doing."
---

1. Load the log; compute consistency (sessions done vs planned), weekly volume, average effort, and key numbers over time.
2. Chart them on the cloud computer and attach the chart.
3. Compare with the goal and say plainly whether they are on track.
4. Suggest one or two changes for next month.

Output:
## <Month> review
- Consistency: x of y sessions
- Volume trend, key numbers
- On track? yes / close / no, and why
- Next month: changes
Only use reported data.
