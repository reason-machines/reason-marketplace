---
name: "Daily check-in"
description: "Use when the daily routine runs or the person reports on a session."
---

1. Ask: did today's session happen? Effort 1-10, how did it feel, sleep, soreness.
2. Log the answer.
3. If missed: move or drop the session and adjust the rest of the week; never double up.
4. If effort was much higher than planned, or they slept badly or feel sore, make tomorrow easier.
5. If they mention pain or illness, advise rest and a professional check.

Output: one short acknowledgement, the logged entry, and tomorrow's session. Keep it under 120 words and encouraging without fuss.
