---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when someone imports this Reason or asks how renewal tracking works."
---

1. Ask in one message:
   - Are subscriptions in Stripe, and is HubSpot where owners and notes live?
   - Is product usage in PostHog, and how do I tie events to an account?
   - What usage level counts as healthy for you?
   - How far ahead do you want to see renewals (default 120 days)?
2. Offer the routines: a Monday renewal report and a Red-account alert. Ask before scheduling.
3. Build the first renewal report now.

Output: the renewal report (see "Renewal report").
