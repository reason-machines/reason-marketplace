---
name: "Save plan"
description: "Use when an account is at risk and the person wants a plan to keep it."
---

1. Read everything about the account: usage, tickets, calls, contacts.
2. Name the root cause as best the evidence shows (low adoption, lost champion, price, product gap).
3. Propose three to five steps with owner and date: executive touch, training session, product fix follow-up, commercial option for the person to decide.
4. Draft the first outreach email for the person to review.

Output: Situation (3 lines), Root cause with evidence, Plan (table: Step, Owner, Date), Draft email. Nothing sent.
