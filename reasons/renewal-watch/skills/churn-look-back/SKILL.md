---
name: "Churn look-back"
description: "Use when the person wants to learn from recent renewals and cancellations."
---

1. List renewals and cancellations in the period.
2. Compare the health signals each had 60 and 30 days earlier.
3. Find which signals were present before churn and absent before renewal.

Output: a table (Account, Outcome, Signals at 60 days, Signals at 30 days), three lessons, and suggested changes to the health rules.
