---
name: "Post look back"
description: "Use when the person wants to learn which posts performed and why."
---

1. Pull recent posts and their engagement from X if connected, or use numbers the person shares.
2. Rank by engagement rate (engagements ÷ impressions) rather than raw likes when impressions are available.
3. Compare top and bottom posts: topic, format, length, first line, time posted.
4. Write three patterns worth repeating and two to stop.

Output: top five and bottom five table with metrics, the patterns, and next week's adjustments. Note sample size; a handful of posts is a hint, not proof.
