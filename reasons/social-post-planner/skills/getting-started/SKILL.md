---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when the person first sets up social planning."
---

1. Ask in one message:
   - Which platforms and how often per week?
   - Share or let me read 10 to 20 of your past posts that you like (X if connected, or paste them).
   - What is coming up in the next two weeks (launches, events, posts)?
   - Topics, words or accounts to avoid?
   - Is Buffer connected for drafts?
2. Write a voice note and confirm it; save to memory.
3. Offer the weekly plan and monthly look back; ask before scheduling.
4. Draft this week's plan now with "Weekly plan".

Output: voice note, routine offer, first week of drafts.
