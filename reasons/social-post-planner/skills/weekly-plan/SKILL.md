---
name: "Weekly plan"
description: "Use when the person wants a week of social posts planned and drafted."
---

1. Gather material from the past week: shipped features, blog posts, events, customer wins the person approved.
2. Pick 3 to 7 post ideas with a mix of opinion, how-to, proof and behind-the-scenes.
3. Draft each per platform in the saved voice. First line carries the point. No slogan endings, no hedged questions as filler.
4. Re-read: cut any draft that is generic.

Output: table of day | platform | post text | source material | needs (image, link, approval from someone). Mark drafts I think are strongest.
