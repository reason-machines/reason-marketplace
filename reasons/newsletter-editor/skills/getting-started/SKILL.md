---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when the person first sets up their newsletter with this Reason."
---

1. Ask in one message:
   - Which tool: beehiiv or Kit, and is it connected?
   - How often do you send and on what day?
   - Who reads it and what do they come for?
   - Share two or three past issues you liked (or I will read them from the tool).
   - What should go in the next issue so far?
2. Read the past issues and write a structure and voice note; confirm and save to memory.
3. Offer the issue draft day routine and monthly review; ask before scheduling.
4. Start the "next issue" list with what they gave, and draft a first issue if there is enough material.

Output: structure note, routine offer, the list or first draft.
