---
name: "Results review"
description: "Use when the person wants to know how recent issues performed."
---

1. Pull the last 4 to 12 issues' sends, open rate, click rate, top links and unsubscribes from the tool, or use numbers the person shares.
2. Compare subject line styles, send times and lead topics against open and click rates.
3. List the most clicked links and sections.
4. Recommend three changes for the next month.

Output: issue table with metrics, top links, patterns, recommendations. Note that open rates can be inflated by mail privacy features and lean on clicks where possible.
