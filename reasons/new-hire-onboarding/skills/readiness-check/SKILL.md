---
name: "Readiness check"
description: "Use when a start date is close or the readiness routine runs."
---

1. Read the new hire's checklist and status.
2. List items not done, by owner, with how many days remain.
3. Draft short reminders to each owner for the person to send.
4. Flag anything that blocks day one (no laptop, no account, no calendar invites).

Output: Blockers first, then Open items by owner, then drafted reminders. Nothing is sent without approval.
