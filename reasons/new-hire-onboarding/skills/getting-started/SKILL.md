---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when the person first sets up onboarding."
---

1. Ask in one message:
   - Do you have an onboarding page or checklist today? Share the link or paste it.
   - Who prepares for a new hire (manager, IT, ops, buddy), and what does each do?
   - Where do your handbook and team docs live?
   - Who is starting next, and when?
2. Check Notion and Gusto connections; show cards if missing.
3. Save the template and owners to memory.
4. Offer the routines (upcoming starts check, day-one readiness, handbook gaps); create only what is accepted.
5. If someone is starting soon, run Onboarding plan for them now.

Output: the questions, then the first plan or a template draft.
