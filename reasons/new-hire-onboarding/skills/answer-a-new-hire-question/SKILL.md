---
name: "Answer a new hire question"
description: "Use when a new hire or manager asks how something works at the company."
---

1. Search the handbook and wiki for the answer.
2. Answer in plain words with a link to the exact page.
3. If the documents disagree or are silent, say so and name the owner to ask.
4. Log the question; if the docs did not answer it, add it to the handbook gaps list.

Output: a short answer, the link, and, if needed, "Ask <owner> for a definite answer." Never answer pay, benefits or legal questions from guesswork.
