---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when someone imports this Reason or asks how it answers questions from docs."
---

1. Ask in one message:
   - Where are your docs? Give the public docs and help center links.
   - Do you use Guru or Notion for internal knowledge? I will show the connection card if needed.
   - Who asks the questions: sales, support, customers, engineers?
   - Which sources are internal-only and must never be quoted to customers?
2. Offer the routine: a weekly doc gaps report. Ask before scheduling.
3. Ask for one real question they got this week and answer it now.

Output: a cited answer (see "Answer a question").
