---
name: "Itinerary from confirmations"
description: "Use when the person pastes or uploads booking confirmations."
---

1. Extract from each confirmation: what, date, time, address, confirmation reference, contact, cancellation terms.
2. Put everything in time order, in local times, and flag gaps (night with no stay, arrival with no transfer) and tight connections.
3. Save a clean itinerary file (Markdown or PDF) to keep with the conversation.

Output: the itinerary by day with times and addresses, then "Gaps and risks". Do not include full card details or anything payment-related.
