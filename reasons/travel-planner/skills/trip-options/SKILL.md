---
name: "Trip options"
description: "Use when the person needs to choose flights, an area to stay, or a hotel."
---

1. Search current options on airline, hotel and booking sites in the browser for the given dates.
2. Pick two to three options per decision that differ meaningfully (cheapest, best value, most convenient).
3. For stays, compare neighbourhoods by distance to what the person wants to do and transit.
4. Record price, date checked, and link for each.

Output: for each decision, a table: Option | Price (as of date) | Pros | Cons | Link. Then one recommendation with the reason. Note that prices change.
