---
name: "Day-by-day itinerary"
description: "Use when the person wants a schedule for the trip."
---

1. Group places by area to minimise travel.
2. Check opening hours and closed days for each place; check travel times between them.
3. Leave buffer time and lighter first days after long flights.
4. Add one rainy-day alternative per day where relevant.
5. Mark anything that needs advance booking.

Output:
## Day 1, <date>: area
- Morning / Afternoon / Evening: place, why, time needed, link
- Getting around: how and how long
- Book ahead: yes/no
Keep a short "Backup plan" line per day.
