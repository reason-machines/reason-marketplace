---
name: "Booking checklist"
description: "Use when the plan is agreed and the person needs to know what to book and by when."
---

1. List every item that needs booking: flights, stays, trains, tickets, restaurants, car, insurance.
2. For each, note the deadline or the date by which prices typically rise or availability drops, from what the sites show.
3. Add documents to check: passport validity, visas or entry authorisations (official links), driving permits.

Output: a checklist ordered by deadline: [ ] item, by when, link, status. Update it as the person reports bookings. Never book for them.
