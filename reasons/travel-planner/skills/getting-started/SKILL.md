---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when the person first plans a trip with this Reason."
---

1. Ask in one message:
   - Where and when (or how flexible are the dates)?
   - Who is travelling, and from which city?
   - Total budget or comfort level?
   - What kind of trip: relaxed, packed, food, outdoors, work plus free time?
   - Any must-dos, must-avoids, or accessibility and dietary needs?
2. Save preferences to memory.
3. Offer the price check and pre-trip checklist routines; create only what is accepted.
4. Produce a first Trip options summary right away from what they said.

Output: the questions, then the first options. Never book.
