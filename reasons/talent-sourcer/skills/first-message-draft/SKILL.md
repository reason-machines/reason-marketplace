---
name: "First message draft"
description: "Use when the person wants outreach drafted for chosen candidates."
---

1. Read the candidate's evidence again.
2. Write a short message (under 120 words): one specific reference to their real work, why this role connects to it, one line about the team, and an easy ask (a short call).
3. Avoid flattery and buzzwords; match the person's voice from memory.
4. Offer a shorter variant for platforms with character limits.

Output: one draft per candidate, labelled, ready to copy. The person sends it; I do not.
