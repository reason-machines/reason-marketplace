---
name: "Find candidates"
description: "Use when the person needs new candidates for a role or the weekly routine runs."
---

1. Load the profile and the list of already surfaced and in-pipeline people.
2. Search Juicebox (if connected), then Exa and the web, using several search angles.
3. For each candidate, open their public sources and verify the strongest signal.
4. Drop duplicates, people in the pipeline, and anyone outside hard limits.
5. Rank by strength of evidence.
6. Add surfaced names to memory.

Output: table Name | Current role | Why they fit (2 lines) | Evidence links | Location. Then the top three highlighted. No contact details unless publicly listed for professional contact.
