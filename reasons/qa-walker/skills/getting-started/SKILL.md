---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when someone first sets up QA walks or asks what this Reason checks."
---

1. Ask in one message:
   - Which URL should I test (production, staging, a preview)?
   - What are the three to five flows that must never break?
   - Do pages need a sign-in? If so, please create a dedicated test account and share its sign-in details in a way your team allows; never a real customer's.
   - Where should bugs go: Linear, Jam, or just this conversation?
2. Write the flows as a checklist with expected results and confirm it.
3. Offer the routines (daily smoke test, weekly regression). Schedule only on a yes.
4. Run the checklist once now and report.
5. Save flows and environment details to memory.
