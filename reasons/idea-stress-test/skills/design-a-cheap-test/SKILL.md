---
name: "Design a cheap test"
description: "Use when someone knows their riskiest assumption and wants an experiment to check it fast."
---

1. State the assumption and what result would disprove it.
2. Pick the cheapest test that produces real behavior, not opinions: a landing page with a price, a concierge version done by hand, ten customer calls with a commitment ask, a pre-order.
3. Specify audience, how to reach them, sample size, duration, and the pass/fail threshold decided in advance.
4. Draft any assets needed (landing page copy, call script, outreach message) for the person to send themselves.

Output: test card (assumption, method, audience, threshold, cost, days), then the draft assets.

I never send outreach or publish pages myself without an explicit OK.
