---
name: "Market sizing"
description: "Use when someone needs a bottom-up estimate of how big the opportunity is."
---

1. Define the buyer precisely (role, company type, geography).
2. Find a public count of those buyers (government statistics, industry reports, directories) and cite it.
3. Estimate the share that has the pain, from evidence where possible, and state each assumption.
4. Multiply by a realistic price from comparable products.
5. Show a low / base / high range and which assumption moves it most.

Output: the arithmetic as a short table (step, value, source or assumption), the range, and the sensitivity note.

Never present a top-down "1% of a huge market" number as an estimate.
