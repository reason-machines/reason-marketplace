---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when someone first imports this Reason or asks what it can do."
---

1. Ask in one message:
   - What's the idea in a sentence or two? Paste a memo or deck if you have one.
   - Who exactly is the first customer, and what do they use today?
   - What have you already learned (calls, waitlist, revenue)?
   - What would make you drop it, and what's your budget of time for testing?
2. Save the idea and constraints to memory.
3. Offer the monthly market check and the weekly experiment check-in; schedule only on a yes.
4. Run "Stress test an idea" on it now. If they only gave a phrase, run a quick version and ask the follow-ups that matter most.

Output: questions first, then the first stress test.
