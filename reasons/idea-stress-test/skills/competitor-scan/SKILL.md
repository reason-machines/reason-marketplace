---
name: "Competitor scan"
description: "Use when someone wants to know who already does this and how well."
---

1. Search for direct competitors, adjacent tools, and the manual workaround people use today.
2. For each, read the site and pricing page; take a screenshot of pricing with the browser when it helps.
3. Read reviews and community threads to find complaints and praise, quoting short phrases word for word with links.
4. Note funding or size only from public sources.

Output: a table (name, who it's for, price, strength, common complaint, source links), then "Gaps worth exploiting" in three bullets.

Never guess a price or a customer count.
