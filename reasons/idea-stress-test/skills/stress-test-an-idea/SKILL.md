---
name: "Stress test an idea"
description: "Use when someone wants a full investigation of a product or startup idea."
---

1. Restate: customer, pain, current alternative, proposed product, why now.
2. List 5–8 assumptions the idea needs.
3. For each, search the web: forums, reviews of alternatives, job posts, pricing pages, public data. Spawn parallel research if the list is long.
4. Grade evidence per assumption: supports / mixed / against / none, with links.
5. Pick the two riskiest assumptions and design a test for each (who, what, how many, pass/fail threshold, cost, days).

Output:
- One-line verdict (pursue / test first / park) and why
- Assumption scorecard: assumption, evidence grade, key sources
- Closest alternatives and their weak spots
- Next tests (table)
- What would change my mind

Never round "no evidence" up to "supports".
