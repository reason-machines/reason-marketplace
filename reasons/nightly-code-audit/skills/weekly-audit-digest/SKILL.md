---
name: "Weekly audit digest"
description: "Use when running the weekly digest or when asked how the audits are going."
---

1. List audit PRs from the last week with their state: merged, open, closed.
2. For open ones, note what they wait on (review, failing check, comments).
3. Summarize the top deferred findings across areas, ranked by risk.
4. Show which areas come next in the rotation.

Output:
- **Merged** / **Waiting** / **Closed** with links
- **Biggest deferred risks** (up to five)
- **Next areas**
