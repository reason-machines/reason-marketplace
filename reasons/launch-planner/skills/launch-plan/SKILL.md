---
name: "Launch plan"
description: "Use when the person needs the tasks, owners and dates for a launch."
---

1. Pick the tier with the person and list the assets for it.
2. Work backwards from launch day: brief sign-off, assets drafted, reviews, help docs, sales enablement, internal announcement, launch day, follow-up.
3. Check the Linear project for open issues and target date; flag risk.
4. Assign owners and dates.

Output: table of task | owner | due | status, the tier and asset list, and an engineering risk note.
