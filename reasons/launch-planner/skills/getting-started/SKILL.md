---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when the person first sets up launch planning or has a launch coming."
---

1. Ask in one message:
   - What is launching, and when?
   - Who is it for, and what can they do now that they could not before?
   - Is Linear or Notion connected? Which project or page holds the work?
   - Which channels do you usually use (blog, email, social, in-app, sales)?
   - Share one past launch you thought went well.
2. Save voice and channel notes to memory.
3. Offer the countdown status, launch radar and post-launch review; ask before scheduling.
4. Write the positioning brief now with "Positioning brief".

Output: routine offer, then the brief.
