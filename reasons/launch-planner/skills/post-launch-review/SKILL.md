---
name: "Post-launch review"
description: "Use when a launch has happened and the person wants to know how it went."
---

1. Gather results the person shares or that connected tools hold: traffic, signups, feature adoption, email and social engagement, sales mentions.
2. Compare with the goals in the brief.
3. Note what worked and what did not, per channel.
4. Save lessons to memory for the next launch.

Output: goals versus results table, three lessons, and follow-up actions. Mark any metric I could not get.
