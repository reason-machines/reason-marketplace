---
name: "Draft launch assets"
description: "Use when the brief is approved and the launch assets need writing."
---

1. From the approved brief, draft: announcement post, customer email, social posts per platform, changelog entry, help article outline, sales one-pager, internal FAQ.
2. Keep the headline and core sentence identical across assets.
3. Check each asset for claims not in the brief.

Output: each asset under its own heading, with word counts, and a list of placeholders the person must fill (screenshots, links, quotes). Drafts only.
