---
name: "Positioning brief"
description: "Use when a launch needs its message agreed before assets are written."
---

1. Read the spec, Linear project and any demo.
2. Write: audience, problem in their words, what is new, why it matters, proof (demo, numbers, quotes you have), objections and answers, what it is not.
3. Write a one-line headline and a three-sentence description, plus two alternatives.
4. Check competitor language for the same idea and avoid sounding identical.

Output: the brief as a one-page Markdown doc, saved to Notion if connected. Mark any proof that is missing.
