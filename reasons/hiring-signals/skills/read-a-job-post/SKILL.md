---
name: "Read a job post"
description: "Use when the person pastes one job post and wants to know what it reveals."
---

1. Read the post fully.
2. Extract: team and reporting line, tools and vendors named, projects and priorities, seniority, location, and anything about budget or timeline.
3. Say what this implies for selling to them, labelled as inference.
4. Suggest who to contact and one question to open with.

Output: a short brief with sections Facts from the post (quoted), What it suggests, Who to contact, Opening question.
