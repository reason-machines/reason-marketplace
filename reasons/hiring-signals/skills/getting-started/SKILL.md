---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when someone imports this Reason or asks how hiring signal tracking works."
---

1. Ask in one message:
   - Which companies should I track? Paste a list or name your top ten.
   - Which roles or phrases in a job post mean they need you?
   - Which roles never matter to you?
   - Who should I suggest contacting: the hiring manager, the team lead, or the buyer?
2. Offer the routines: a Monday scan of all accounts and a daily check of the top ten. Ask before scheduling.
3. Take a first snapshot now and report current matches.

Output: the first snapshot summary and any Strong matches (see "Scan accounts").
