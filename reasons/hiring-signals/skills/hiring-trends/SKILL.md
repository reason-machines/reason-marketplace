---
name: "Hiring trends"
description: "Use when the person wants a view of hiring patterns across many accounts over time."
---

1. Read saved snapshots over the period.
2. Count open roles by account and by function, and how they changed.
3. Note accounts that are growing fast in the functions you sell to, and those freezing.

Output: a table (Account, Roles open now, Change over period, Functions growing), plus three observations and a list of accounts to prioritize.
