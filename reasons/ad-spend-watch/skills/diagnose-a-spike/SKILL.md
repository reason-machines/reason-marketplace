---
name: "Diagnose a spike"
description: "Use when a campaign's cost, spend or conversions changed sharply and the person wants to know why."
---

1. Narrow the change: which day it started, which campaign, ad set, ad, audience, device or placement moved most.
2. Check known causes in order: a budget or bid change, new or paused ads, audience or targeting edits, landing page change or outage (visit the page), tracking drop (all channels falling together), auction pressure (higher cost per click with stable click-through), creative fatigue (falling click-through, rising frequency).
3. Rate each cause likely, possible or ruled out, with evidence.

Output: a short diagnosis with the evidence table, the most likely cause, and one or two fixes with expected effect. Say plainly when the data cannot decide.
