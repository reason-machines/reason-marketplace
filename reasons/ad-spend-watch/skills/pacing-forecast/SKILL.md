---
name: "Pacing forecast"
description: "Use when the person asks whether a channel or campaign will over- or under-spend this month."
---

1. Get month-to-date spend per channel and campaign, and the monthly budget.
2. Project month-end spend from the last seven days' daily average, and also from the month-to-date average; show both.
3. Flag anything projected above 105% or below 90% of budget.
4. Propose daily budget changes that would land each channel on target.

Output: table with channel | budget | spent | projected (7-day) | projected (MTD) | status | suggested daily budget. Keep a short note on assumptions (for example a promotion next week).
