---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when the person first sets up spend monitoring or asks how it works."
---

1. Ask in one message:
   - Which ad platforms do you run, and is Adspirer or Supermetrics connected? Otherwise, can you upload a recent export?
   - Monthly budget per channel?
   - Your target cost per conversion or return on ad spend, and what counts as a conversion?
   - How big a move should alert you (for example cost per conversion up 30% day over day)?
   - Who should hear about it, and how early in the day?
2. Save budgets, targets and thresholds to memory.
3. Offer the morning check, mid-month pacing and weekly summary; ask before creating any routine.
4. Run "Daily spend check" on the data available now.

Output: the confirmed rules, the routine offer and the first check.
