---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when someone imports this Reason or asks how deal inspection works."
---

1. Ask in one message:
   - Which qualification framework do you use, and what must be true at each stage?
   - Are deals in HubSpot and calls in Gong, or will you paste transcripts?
   - Which pipeline and which stage changes matter most to inspect?
   - Who should see the results?
2. Offer the routine: a weekly inspection of deals that moved stage. Ask before scheduling.
3. Inspect one deal now from what they gave.

Output: a deal scorecard (see "Inspect one deal").
