---
name: "Commit deal audit"
description: "Use when the forecast is being called and commit deals need checking."
---

1. Take every deal in the commit category for the period.
2. Check the hard items: economic buyer confirmed, decision process known, paper process started, a dated next step.
3. Flag any commit deal missing two or more.

Output: a table (Deal, Amount, Economic buyer, Process, Paper, Next step, Flag), and a one-paragraph summary of how solid the commit number is.
