---
name: "Promote or close"
description: "Use when deciding what to do with existing prototype PRs."
---

1. List open prototype PRs with age, last activity and what each tested.
2. For each, propose: close (learned what we needed), promote (turn into a real spec), or keep.
3. For promote, draft the follow-up spec outline and what production-ready work remains (tests, real data, edge cases).
4. Wait for the person's choices.
5. Close or label PRs only after an OK, leaving a comment with what was learned.

Output: table (PR, age, tested, proposal, reason).
