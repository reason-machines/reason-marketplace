---
name: "Write a brief"
description: "Use when the person wants a writer-ready brief for a keyword or topic."
---

1. Read the top eight results for the primary keyword. Note format, headings, length and freshness.
2. List what all of them cover (must have) and what none cover well (our angle).
3. Draft the outline: H1, H2s, H3s, with one line each on what the section answers.
4. List questions to answer in one or two sentences near the top of a section, so answer engines can quote them.
5. Add facts to include with sources, internal links (from the person's site), and external sources worth citing.

Output (Markdown):
- Target: primary keyword, variants, intent, reader
- Angle: one sentence
- Outline
- Direct answers to include
- Sources and internal links
- Length range, with the reason
- Do not: things to avoid (claims, competitors, tone)
