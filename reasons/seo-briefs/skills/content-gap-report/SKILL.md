---
name: "Content gap report"
description: "Use when the person wants to see which topics competitors rank for that they do not."
---

1. With Ahrefs or Semrush, pull keyword gaps between the person's domain and each competitor. Without them, ask for exports or compare the competitors' sitemaps and top pages using the browser, and label the result as directional.
2. Filter to keywords that match the person's product and audience.
3. Cluster them (see "Cluster keywords") and estimate value: volume, intent, and fit with the product.
4. Pick the top ten clusters.

Output:
- Table: cluster | competitor ranking page | their position | our page (if any) | priority
- Three quick wins (existing pages that only need an update)
- The data source and date for every number.
