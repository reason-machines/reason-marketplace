---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when the person first sets up this Reason or asks what it does."
---

1. Ask in one message:
   - Your domain, and two or three competitor domains?
   - Which market and language?
   - Is Ahrefs or Semrush connected, or will you paste exports?
   - Who reads the briefs (in-house writers, freelancers, yourself) and where should they go?
   - Paste a keyword list or a few topics to start.
2. Save domain, competitors, market and brief destination to memory.
3. Offer the monthly opportunities report and weekly brief batch; ask before scheduling.
4. Run "Cluster keywords" on what they gave, then write one full brief for the strongest cluster.

Output: the cluster table, one complete brief, and the routine offer.
