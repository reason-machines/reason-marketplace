---
name: "Final proofread"
description: "Use when the piece is nearly done and needs a last check before it goes out."
---

1. Check spelling, grammar, punctuation, consistent names, numbers and dates, and broken or missing links.
2. Check the title and opening line one more time.
3. Read it as the intended reader and note any last confusion.

Output: a numbered list of fixes with location, then the corrected text. If it is ready, say "Ready to go" plainly. Never send or publish it.
