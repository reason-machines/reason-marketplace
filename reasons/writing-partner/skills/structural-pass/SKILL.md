---
name: "Structural pass"
description: "Use when a full draft exists and the person wants to know if it works as a whole."
---

1. Read the whole draft once without editing.
2. Write a reverse outline: one line per paragraph saying what it does.
3. Check: is the main point clear in the first few paragraphs? Does each section earn its place? Is anything repeated or out of order?
4. Recommend moves, cuts and additions at the section level.

Output:
## What works (2-3 bullets)
## Reverse outline
## Recommended changes (numbered, each with the reason)
Do not line-edit in this pass.
