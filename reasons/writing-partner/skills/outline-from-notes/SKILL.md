---
name: "Outline from notes"
description: "Use when the person has an idea or messy notes and needs a plan before drafting."
---

1. Read the notes and pull out the main claims, examples and questions.
2. Propose a one-sentence thesis and ask whether it is right.
3. Order the points into sections that a reader can follow, with the strongest point placed where it lands hardest.
4. Note where evidence or an example is missing.

Output:
**Thesis:** one sentence
**Outline:** numbered sections, each with a one-line purpose and the notes that belong there
**Gaps:** what is still needed
Do not write prose yet unless asked.
