---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when the person first starts working with this Reason."
---

1. Ask in one message:
   - What are you writing right now, and who is it for?
   - What should the reader think, feel or do after reading?
   - Can you share one or two pieces you wrote that sound like you?
   - Do you want heavy edits or a light touch?
2. Read the samples and save a short voice profile to memory (tone, sentence length, habits).
3. Offer the optional routines (writing session nudge, weekly draft review); create only what is accepted.
4. If they shared a draft, do a first Structural pass now. If they shared only an idea, run Outline from notes.

Output: the questions, then the first piece of real work.
