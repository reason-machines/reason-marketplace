---
name: "Line edit"
description: "Use when the structure is settled and the person wants sentence-level tightening."
---

1. Edit paragraph by paragraph for clarity and rhythm: cut filler, fix vague words, break long sentences, keep the person's voice profile.
2. Do not change meaning. Flag anything that might change meaning instead of changing it.
3. Keep a change log.

Output: the edited text, then "Changes:" with up to ten notable edits as "before -> after (why)". If the person asks, give a full side-by-side. Never add new claims.
