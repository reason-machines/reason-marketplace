---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when the person first sets up lead scoring and routing."
---

1. Ask in one message:
   - Is HubSpot connected, or will you upload exports? Is Clay connected for enrichment?
   - Describe your ideal customer: company size, industry, region, roles.
   - How should leads be routed: territories, round robin, named accounts?
   - What is your response-time target for a new lead?
   - Who are the owners, and anyone currently out?
2. Write the scoring model (fit and intent weights) and routing rules back to the person in a small table; confirm and save to memory.
3. Offer the morning triage, stuck lead alert and weekly funnel note; ask before scheduling.
4. Run "Triage new leads" now on the current queue.

Output: scoring model, routing rules, routine offer, first triage.
