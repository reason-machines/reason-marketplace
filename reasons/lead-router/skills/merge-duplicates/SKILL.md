---
name: "Merge duplicates"
description: "Use when duplicate leads or contacts need to be found and merged."
---

1. Find candidates: exact email match; same company domain with similar name; same phone.
2. For each group pick the record to keep (most activity, oldest, has an owner) and list the fields to carry over.
3. Mark uncertain matches separately.
4. On explicit approval, merge through HubSpot one group at a time and read back the result.

Output: table of group | records | keep | fields to carry | confidence. Never merge uncertain matches without a separate yes.
