---
name: "Stuck leads"
description: "Use when the person wants to see leads that have not been followed up in time."
---

1. List open leads with no logged activity past the response-time target.
2. Group by owner and show age.
3. For each, suggest the next action (reassign, nudge owner, close as unqualified) with a reason.
4. Draft a short note to each owner if asked, for the person to send.

Output: table owner | lead | age | score | suggested action. Drafts only; never contact leads or owners myself.
