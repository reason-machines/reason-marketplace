---
name: "Funnel note"
description: "Use when the person wants a summary of inbound lead flow and quality."
---

1. Count leads by source and week for the period.
2. Compute qualified rate, median time to first touch, and conversion to meeting where data allows.
3. Compare to the previous period.
4. Write three observations and one suggested change.

Output: source table with counts and rates, the deltas, and the observations, with the data source and date range.
