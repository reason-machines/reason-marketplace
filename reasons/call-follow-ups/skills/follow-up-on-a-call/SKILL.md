---
name: "Follow up on a call"
description: "Use when the person had a call and wants the follow-up email and notes."
---

1. Get the transcript and identify the account and deal.
2. Extract goals, pains, questions owed, promises and next steps with dates.
3. Draft the email: under 150 words, recap in three bullets using their phrasing, owed answers, one clear next step with a date.
4. Draft the CRM note and any field changes.
5. Ask for approval; on yes, save the email as a draft (or send only if they explicitly say send) and apply the CRM update.

Output: Email (subject and body), CRM note, Field changes (table: Field, From, To), Open promises (list with owner and due date).
