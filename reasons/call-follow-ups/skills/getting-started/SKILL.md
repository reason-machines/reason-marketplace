---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when someone imports this Reason or asks how call follow-ups work."
---

1. Ask in one message:
   - Where do your call notes live: Granola, Fireflies, or will you paste transcripts?
   - Should I save follow-ups as drafts in Outlook (Microsoft 365 Mail), or just show them here?
   - Is HubSpot your CRM, and should I prepare deal updates too?
   - Paste one follow-up email you liked, so I match your voice.
2. Offer the routine: weekday evening follow-ups for the day's calls. Ask before scheduling.
3. Take the most recent call and draft its follow-up now.

Output: the follow-up package (see "Follow up on a call").
