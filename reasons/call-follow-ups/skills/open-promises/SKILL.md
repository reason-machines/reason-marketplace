---
name: "Open promises"
description: "Use when the person wants to know what they or their customers still owe."
---

1. Read saved promises from memory and scan recent transcripts for new ones.
2. Check mail threads for whether each was fulfilled.
3. Mark each Open, Done or Overdue.

Output: a table (Account, Promise, Who owes it, Due, Status) sorted by overdue first, with a suggested one-line nudge for overdue customer items.
