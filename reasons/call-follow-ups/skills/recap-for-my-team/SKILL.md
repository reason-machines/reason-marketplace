---
name: "Recap for my team"
description: "Use when the person wants an internal summary of a call to share with colleagues."
---

1. Read the transcript.
2. Write a short internal recap: who attended, what they want, deal impact, risks, asks for the team (product, legal, security).
3. Keep customer quotes exact.

Output: a Slack-ready message of under 120 words with bullet points, plus a list of asks with owners. Posting to Slack only if connected and after approval.
