---
name: "Match feedback to tickets"
description: "Use when someone wants customer evidence attached to roadmap items or issues."
---

1. Read the open issues or roadmap items the person names.
2. For each, find matching feedback and count distinct customers.
3. Find feedback themes with no ticket at all.
4. Prepare a short evidence comment per ticket (count, two quotes with links).
5. Show the comments and post them only after an OK.

Output: table (ticket, customers, strongest quote), list of unticketed themes, and the draft comments.

Never post comments or create tickets without approval.
