---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when someone first imports this Reason or asks what it can do."
---

1. Ask in one message:
   - Where does customer feedback live (Enterpret, Intercom, Plain, a survey tool, app reviews, Slack)?
   - What window should the first digest cover?
   - Do you have a theme list or tags already? Any accounts that matter most?
   - Who reads the digest, and what decisions does it feed?
2. Save sources, accounts and audience to memory. Show connection cards for missing tools, or ask for an export.
3. Offer the weekly digest, release follow-up and monthly asks routines; schedule only on a yes.
4. Produce a first digest now with the "Build a digest" playbook from whatever is available.

Output: questions, then the first digest.
