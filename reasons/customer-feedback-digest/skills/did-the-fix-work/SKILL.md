---
name: "Did the fix work"
description: "Use when a fix shipped and someone wants to know whether complaints dropped."
---

1. Get the theme, the ship date and the release link.
2. Count distinct customers raising the theme per week for four weeks before and every week after.
3. Read post-release mentions to see if they're about the old problem or a new variant.
4. Note customers who complained before and could be told it's fixed.

Output: a small weekly table, a verdict (fixed / improved / unchanged / worse), and a list of customers to follow up with plus a draft reply for each, to be sent only on your OK.
