---
name: "Theme deep dive"
description: "Use when someone wants everything about one specific complaint or request."
---

1. Search all sources for the theme using several phrasings, not just one keyword.
2. List every distinct customer who raised it, with date, plan if known and link.
3. Group the variations: what exactly people want, the workaround they use, how upset they are.
4. Check existing tickets (Linear or GitHub if connected) for the same ask.

Output: a short summary, a table of mentions (customer, date, quote, link), the variations, and the matching tickets. End with a suggested next step (spec it, reply to the customers, or watch).
