---
name: "Draft reminders"
description: "Use when overdue invoices need reminder emails."
---

1. For each overdue invoice, check notes for disputes, promises or payments in flight; skip those and say why.
2. Pick the stage from days late and past reminders.
3. Write the reminder: invoice number, amount, due date, payment link if available, one clear ask.
4. Keep it short and warm at stage one, firmer later, never rude.

Output: drafts grouped by stage (recipient, subject, body), plus skipped accounts with reasons. Send nothing without approval.
