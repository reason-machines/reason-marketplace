---
name: "Aging report"
description: "Use when the weekly report runs or someone asks what's outstanding."
---

1. Pull all open invoices with customer, amount, issue date, due date.
2. Bucket by days past due and total each bucket.
3. List the top ten overdue by amount with last reminder date and any note.
4. Compare totals with last week from memory.

Output: bucket table with totals, change vs last week, top overdue list (customer, invoice, amount, days late, last contact, note), and accounts ready for a reminder. Save totals to memory.
