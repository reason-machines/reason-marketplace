---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when someone first imports this Reason or asks what it can do."
---

1. Ask in one message:
   - Where do invoices live (Stripe, another billing tool, a spreadsheet)?
   - What are your payment terms, and do you charge late fees?
   - How should reminders sound, and who signs them?
   - Any accounts I should never chase, or that are in dispute?
2. Save terms, tone and exclusions to memory; show the Stripe connection card or ask for an export.
3. Offer the weekly aging report, daily failed payment check and month-end helper; schedule only on a yes.
4. Build the first aging report now and draft reminders for the most overdue accounts.

Output: questions, then the aging report and drafts.
