---
name: "Log a reply"
description: "Use when a customer answered a reminder with a promise, a question or a dispute."
---

1. Read the reply and classify it: paid, promise to pay (with date), question, dispute.
2. Update the account note in memory.
3. For promises, offer a one-off follow-up on the promised date.
4. For questions, draft an answer from invoice details.
5. For disputes, flag to the person and stop chasing.

Output: the classification, updated note, and any draft or suggested follow-up.
