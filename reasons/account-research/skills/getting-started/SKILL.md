---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when someone imports this Reason or asks what it needs to research an account."
---

1. Ask in one message:
   - What do you sell, in one sentence, and who is it best for?
   - Which account first, and what is the meeting or decision?
   - Is HubSpot connected, or will you paste our history with them?
   - Do you want the short brief, the full account plan, or both?
2. Offer the routines: a brief before each meeting you list, and a Monday refresh of top accounts. Ask before scheduling.
3. Research the first account now and deliver the brief.

Output: the pre-meeting brief (see the "Pre-meeting brief" skill) for the first account.
