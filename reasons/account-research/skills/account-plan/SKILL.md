---
name: "Account plan"
description: "Use when the person wants a fuller plan for winning or growing a strategic account."
---

1. Start from the latest brief, or write one first.
2. Map the organization: business units, likely buyers, champions, blockers, with confidence for each.
3. List use cases where we fit, ranked by value to them and evidence.
4. Identify the gaps: people we do not know, facts we lack.
5. Propose a 30-day plan with owners and dates.

Output: a Markdown account plan with sections Overview, Org map (table: Person, Role, Stance, Confidence, Source), Opportunities, Risks, Gaps, 30-day plan (table: Action, Owner, Date). Attached as a file.
