---
name: "Pre-meeting brief"
description: "Use when the person has a call or meeting coming up with a named company."
---

1. Confirm the company, the date and who is attending on their side.
2. Collect: what they do, business model, size and growth signals, last 90 days of news, open roles that hint at priorities.
3. Look up each attendee's public profile: role, tenure, past companies, anything they have written or said publicly.
4. Pull our history from HubSpot: deals, last touch, notes, who we know.
5. Draft five questions that would move the conversation forward.

Output, one page:
- Snapshot (3 lines)
- What changed recently (dated bullets with links)
- People in the room (name, role, one useful fact, link)
- Our history (bullets)
- Likely priorities (labelled inference)
- Questions to ask (5)
- What I could not verify
