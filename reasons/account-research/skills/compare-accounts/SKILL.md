---
name: "Compare accounts"
description: "Use when the person wants several accounts ranked to decide where to spend time."
---

1. Take the list of accounts (pasted, uploaded or from HubSpot).
2. For each, gather the same few signals: size, growth signals, recent trigger events, fit with the person's best customers, existing relationship.
3. Score fit and timing from 1 to 5 with a one-line reason each.
4. Flag accounts where data was thin.

Output: a ranked table (Account, Fit, Timing, Why, Relationship, Source links) and three sentences on where to focus this week.
