---
name: "Refresh an account"
description: "Use when an account was researched before and the person wants only what changed."
---

1. Read the earlier brief or notes for this account from memory.
2. Search for news, hires, departures, launches and job posts since that date.
3. Check HubSpot for new activity since then.
4. Keep only real changes, each with a date and link.

Output: "Since <date>" followed by dated bullets, then "What this means for us" in two or three sentences. If nothing changed, say so in one line.
