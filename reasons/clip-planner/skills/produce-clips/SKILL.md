---
name: "Produce clips"
description: "Use when the person approves a clip plan and wants the actual clip files made."
---

1. With OpusClip or Descript connected, create the clips from the plan's timestamps and caption style; otherwise use ffmpeg on my computer to cut, reframe (crop to 9:16 or 1:1), and burn in the SRT captions.
2. Check each output: starts and ends cleanly (no clipped words), captions in sync, faces in frame after reframing, audio level consistent.
3. Name files clearly: date, clip number, platform, ratio.
4. Keep the files with the conversation and list them.

Output: list of files with length and ratio, plus any clip that failed a check and why. Never post.
