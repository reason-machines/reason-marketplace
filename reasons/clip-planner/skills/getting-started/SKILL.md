---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when the person first sets up clip planning or shares their first recording."
---

1. Ask in one message:
   - Which platforms do clips go to, and what lengths and aspect ratios?
   - Is OpusClip or Descript connected, or should I give an edit list and simple cuts?
   - Caption style: burned in or separate, any brand fonts or colours?
   - Who are the speakers, and how are their names spelled?
   - Share the first recording or transcript.
2. Save the platform and style rules to memory.
3. Offer a weekly check for new recordings and a clip calendar; ask before scheduling.
4. Run "Find the moments" on the first recording.

Output: rules confirmed, routine offer, first clip plan.
