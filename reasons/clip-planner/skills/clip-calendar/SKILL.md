---
name: "Clip calendar"
description: "Use when the person wants approved clips scheduled into a posting plan."
---

1. List approved clips and their platforms.
2. Spread them over the coming two to four weeks, avoiding two clips from the same moment back to back and matching the person's usual posting days.
3. Pair each slot with its copy.

Output: a calendar table (date | platform | clip | title | description). If Buffer or another scheduler is connected through another Reason, note it; this Reason does not post or schedule.
