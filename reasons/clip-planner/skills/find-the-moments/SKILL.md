---
name: "Find the moments"
description: "Use when the person shares a long recording and wants the best clip candidates."
---

1. Get a timestamped transcript (provided, from a connected tool, or by transcribing the file).
2. Mark candidate moments: standalone points, stories, strong opinions, how-tos, quotable lines.
3. Score each 1 to 5 on hook strength, standalone clarity and relevance to the audience.
4. Pick the top 5 to 10. Set in and out points that start on the hook and end on the payoff, 15 to 90 seconds unless rules say otherwise.

Output: table of clip # | in | out | length | speaker | hook line (exact quote) | why it works | platforms. Then the full transcript excerpt for each pick.
