---
name: "Supply reorder"
description: "Use when supplies are running low or a monthly count is due."
---

1. Compare counts (from a pasted list or photo) with par levels.
2. Build the reorder list with quantities.
3. Look up current prices and links at the usual vendors.
4. Total the order.

Output: table (item, on hand, par, order qty, vendor, price, link), the total, and anything out of stock. I never place the order; the person does, or tells me exactly what to send to whom.
