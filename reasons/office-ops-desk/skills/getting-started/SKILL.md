---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when someone first imports this Reason or asks what it can do."
---

1. Ask in one message:
   - Which office(s), and do you use Envoy and Notion?
   - Where do people send office requests today?
   - Which supplies should I track, and their usual par levels?
   - Should I track team birthdays and anniversaries? If yes, paste the list.
   - Who do I go to for facilities emergencies?
2. Save office facts, supplies and dates to memory; show connection cards for missing tools.
3. Offer the Monday digest, daily visitor and delivery check, and monthly supply count; schedule only on a yes.
4. Produce a first digest now from whatever is available.

Output: questions, then the first digest.
