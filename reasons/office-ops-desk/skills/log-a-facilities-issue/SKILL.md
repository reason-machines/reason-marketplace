---
name: "Log a facilities issue"
description: "Use when something in the office is broken or needs fixing."
---

1. Capture what, where, since when, urgency and a photo if given.
2. Check the tracker for a duplicate.
3. Find the right vendor or contact from memory.
4. Draft the request to the vendor or landlord with all details.
5. Log it with status and a follow-up date.

Output: the logged item and the draft request. Send only after approval. Urgent safety issues: tell the person immediately at the top of the reply.
