---
name: "Visitor prep"
description: "Use when guests, candidates or a group are coming to the office."
---

1. Get names, host, date, time and purpose.
2. Check Envoy for existing registrations; draft invites for missing ones.
3. Prepare the welcome note: address, entrance, parking, wifi, host contact.
4. List room, food or badge needs and who handles each.

Output: a visitor checklist and the welcome note draft. Nothing is sent or registered without approval.
