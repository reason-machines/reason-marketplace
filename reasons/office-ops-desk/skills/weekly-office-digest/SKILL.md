---
name: "Weekly office digest"
description: "Use when the Monday digest runs or someone asks what's happening at the office this week."
---

1. Pull this week's expected visitors and deliveries.
2. List open facilities issues with age and owner.
3. Check supplies against par levels.
4. List team dates in the next two weeks.

Output (short sections): Visitors, Deliveries, Open issues (oldest first), Low supplies with a reorder list, Team dates with a suggested card or treat, Needs your decision. Offer a Slack-ready version of the team-facing parts.
