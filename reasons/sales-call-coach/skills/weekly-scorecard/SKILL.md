---
name: "Weekly scorecard"
description: "Use when the person wants a roll-up of a rep's or team's calls over a week."
---

1. Pull the week's calls from Gong or Fathom, or take the pasted set.
2. Review each one briefly against the rubric.
3. Average the scores per area and compare with last week from memory.
4. Pick the best moment of the week to share as an example.

Output: a table per rep (Area, This week, Last week, Change), the best moment quoted with timestamp, and one focus for next week. Saves the scores to memory.
