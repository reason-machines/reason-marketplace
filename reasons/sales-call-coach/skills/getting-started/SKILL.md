---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when someone imports this Reason or asks how call coaching works."
---

1. Ask in one message:
   - Are your calls in Gong or Fathom, or will you paste transcripts?
   - Do you have a sales framework or rubric I should score against?
   - Whose calls am I coaching: just you, or a team (names)?
   - What is the one thing you most want to get better at?
2. Offer the routines: a Friday weekly scorecard and a next-morning review of long calls. Ask before scheduling.
3. Ask for one recent call and review it now.

Output: a full call review (see "Review one call") for the first call.
