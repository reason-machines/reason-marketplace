---
name: "Prep for the next call"
description: "Use when the person has a follow-up call and wants a plan based on the last one."
---

1. Read the last call with this account.
2. List what the buyer asked for, what we promised and what is still unanswered.
3. Note objections raised and whether they were resolved.
4. Draft an agenda and three questions that close the gaps.

Output: Open items (bullets with quotes), Objections still open, Proposed agenda (3 to 5 lines), Questions to ask (3).
