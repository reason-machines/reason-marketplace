---
name: "Review one call"
description: "Use when the person shares a call transcript or recording and wants feedback."
---

1. Read the full transcript. Note the call type and the buyer's stated goals.
2. Compute talk ratio and longest monologue from the transcript timestamps when they exist.
3. Score each rubric area 1 to 5 with one supporting quote and timestamp.
4. Mark the three most important moments and what happened next.
5. Choose two fixes. For one of them, rewrite the exact exchange as it could have gone.

Output:
- Summary (2 lines) and overall score
- Scorecard table (Area, Score, Evidence quote, Timestamp)
- Moments that mattered (3)
- Two things to change next call
- One rewrite: what was said / what to try
