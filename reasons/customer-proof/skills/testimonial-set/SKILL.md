---
name: "Testimonial set"
description: "Use when the person needs short quotes for a website, deck or ad."
---

1. Pick the strongest approved or draft quotes from the library.
2. Cut each to under 40 words using an ellipsis where trimmed, without changing meaning.
3. Group by theme (speed, savings, support, ease).

Output: a list by theme: quote, name, title, company, status. Draft quotes are clearly marked as needing approval.
