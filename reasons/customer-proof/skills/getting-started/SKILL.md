---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when someone imports this Reason or asks how it finds and writes customer proof."
---

1. Ask in one message:
   - Where are your calls and notes: Gong, Granola, or pasted transcripts?
   - What do you need most: case studies, short testimonials, stats for the website, slides?
   - Do you have a case study format or brand voice guide? Paste or upload it.
   - Which customers have already agreed to be referenced?
2. Offer the routine: a weekly scan of new calls for quotable moments. Ask before scheduling.
3. Scan recent calls or the pasted material now.

Output: the first proof finds (see "Find quotable moments").
