---
name: "Write a case study"
description: "Use when the person picks a customer and wants a full case study draft."
---

1. Gather every call, note and number for the customer.
2. Outline: headline with the result, the customer in one line, the problem, why they chose us, what they did, results, a closing quote.
3. Write 500 to 800 words in the brand voice. Customer words stay exact.
4. List every claim and its source, and items needing confirmation.

Output: the draft as a Markdown file attached, plus a Confirmations list (claim, needed from whom). A short approval email to the customer is drafted, not sent.
