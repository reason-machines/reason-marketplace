---
name: "Find quotable moments"
description: "Use when the person wants usable praise and results pulled from calls or notes."
---

1. Read the chosen calls or documents fully.
2. Mark moments with specific praise, a clear before-and-after, or a number.
3. Skip vague compliments ("it's great") unless nothing better exists.
4. Save each to the proof library as a draft.

Output: a table (Customer, Speaker and role, Exact quote, Source and timestamp, Type: praise / result / story, Approval status).
