---
name: "Renewal prep"
description: "Use when a contract or subscription is about to renew and someone wants to negotiate or decide."
---

1. Read the current plan, price, seats and usage.
2. Check public pricing and comparable tools.
3. Decide a target: drop seats, change plan, ask for a discount, or switch.
4. Draft the email to the vendor with the specific ask and the facts behind it.

Output: a one-page brief (current, usage, options, recommendation, walk-away point) and the draft email. Nothing is sent without approval.
