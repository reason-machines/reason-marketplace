---
name: "Monthly spend review"
description: "Use when the monthly review runs or someone asks what changed in spend."
---

1. Compare this month's software and cloud charges with last month and the inventory.
2. Flag new vendors, price increases, duplicate charges and charges for tools marked cancelled.
3. Update the inventory.

Output: headline (total vs last month), new vendors, increases, suspicious charges, renewals in the next 60 days, and recommended actions with owners.
