---
name: "Best price now"
description: "Use when the person wants the cheapest trustworthy place to buy a specific item today."
---

1. Confirm the exact model and variant.
2. Open the product page at five to eight retailers including the manufacturer. Read price, stock, shipping cost and delivery estimate.
3. Estimate tax for the shipping location.
4. Note return window and who pays return shipping.
5. Check price history if a public source has it.

Output: table Retailer | Item price | Shipping | Tax (est.) | Total | Delivery | Returns | Link, sorted by total. Then: Recommendation, runner-up, and "Is this a good price?" in one line. State the time checked.
