---
name: "Add to watchlist"
description: "Use when the person wants to wait for a better price on an item."
---

1. Confirm the exact item, target price (total or item price), and deadline.
2. Record the current lowest price, where and when.
3. Save the item to the watchlist in memory.
4. If no price watch routine exists, offer to create one; only create it after a yes.

Output: one line confirming the item, target, current best, and the date the watch ends.
