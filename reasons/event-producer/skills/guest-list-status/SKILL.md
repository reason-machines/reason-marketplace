---
name: "Guest list status"
description: "Use when the person wants the guest list updated or summarized."
---

1. Merge any new registration export or replies the person shares into the list; remove duplicates.
2. Count invited, registered, confirmed, declined, waitlist.
3. Collect dietary needs and accessibility requests into a summary for the caterer and venue.
4. List VIPs not yet confirmed.

Output: counts, the dietary summary, the VIP follow-up list, and drafted reminders for unconfirmed guests for the person to send.
