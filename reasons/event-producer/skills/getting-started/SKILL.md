---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when the person starts planning an event with this Reason."
---

1. Ask in one message:
   - What is the event, its goal and the date?
   - City, venue (if chosen) and headcount?
   - Budget, and who approves spending?
   - Where should the plan live: Notion, Airtable, or files here?
   - Paste any guest list, vendor quotes or notes you have.
2. Write the one-page brief and confirm it; save to memory.
3. Offer the weekly status and reminder drafts; ask before scheduling.
4. Build the backwards plan now with "Event plan".

Output: brief, routine offer, the plan.
