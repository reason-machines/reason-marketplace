---
name: "Run of show"
description: "Use when the person needs the minute-by-minute schedule and day-of checklist."
---

1. Lay out the day from load-in to teardown in 5 to 15 minute blocks: setup, doors, welcome, sessions, food, networking, close.
2. For each block list who is responsible, what is needed (AV, mic, slides), and the cue to move on.
3. Write the day-of checklist: what to bring, who to call if something fails, dietary labels, accessibility needs, emergency contacts.

Output: the run of show table (time | block | owner | needs | cue) and the checklist, ready to print.
