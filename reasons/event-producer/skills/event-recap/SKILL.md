---
name: "Event recap"
description: "Use when the event is over and the person wants a recap and follow-ups."
---

1. Gather attendance (check-in list or count), spend from vendor invoices, and notes.
2. Compute show rate, cost per attendee and cost per follow-up meeting if known.
3. List follow-ups owed: thank-you notes, speaker notes, leads to send to sales.
4. Write what to keep and change next time.

Output: recap summary, numbers table, follow-up list with drafted thank-you notes, and lessons saved to memory.
