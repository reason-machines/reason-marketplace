---
name: "Getting started"
description: "Use when the person first sets up this Reason or asks what it can do."
---

1. Check whether Microsoft 365 Mail and Microsoft 365 Calendar are connected. If not, show the connection cards and offer to work from pasted threads meanwhile.
2. Ask these in one message:
   - What are your working hours and time zone?
   - Who are the people whose mail should always come first?
   - How do you usually sign off, and should replies be short or detailed?
   - How many meetings a day is too many, and which blocks are protected?
   - Should I run a morning triage on weekdays, and at what time?
3. Save the answers to memory.
4. Offer the routines (morning triage, end-of-day sweep, weekly look-ahead, follow-up nudge). Create only the ones the person says yes to.
5. Run a first triage right away on the last 24 hours of mail and the next two days of calendar.

Output: a short welcome, the questions, then the first triage in the Morning triage format. Never send or book anything during setup.
