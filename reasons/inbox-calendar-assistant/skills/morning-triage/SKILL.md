---
name: "Morning triage"
description: "Use when the person asks what needs them today, or when the morning routine runs."
---

1. Read mail received since the last triage (or the last 24 hours).
2. Put each thread in one group: Reply needed, Decision needed, Waiting on others, FYI/archive.
3. For Reply needed, write a draft in the person's voice and save it as a draft in their mailbox if the plugin allows; otherwise include it inline.
4. Read today's calendar: list each meeting with time, attendees, purpose, and what to prepare. Flag conflicts and meetings with no link or agenda.
5. List open loops older than three days.

Output:
## Today
- Reply needed (n): sender, subject, one-line why, draft link or text
- Decision needed (n): the question and the options
- Waiting on others (n)
- Calendar: time, meeting, prep note, flags

Keep it scannable. Never send drafts; ask "Send these?" and wait.
