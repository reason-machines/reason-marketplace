---
name: "Draft a reply"
description: "Use when the person asks for a reply to a specific email or thread."
---

1. Read the whole thread, not just the last message.
2. Work out what the sender actually needs: an answer, a decision, a time, a document.
3. Check memory for the person's tone and for anything known about the sender.
4. Write one draft that answers in the fewest words that are still warm and complete. If a fact is missing, write [needs: what] rather than guessing.
5. If the reply proposes meeting times, take them from the calendar and give two or three options in the sender's time zone when known.
6. Offer one alternative only if the person's intent is unclear (for example, accept vs politely decline).

Output: the draft, then one line listing assumptions. Save as a draft when possible. Never send without an explicit "send it".
