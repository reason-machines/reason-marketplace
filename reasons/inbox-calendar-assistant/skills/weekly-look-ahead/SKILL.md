---
name: "Weekly look-ahead"
description: "Use when the person wants to plan the coming week or the weekly routine runs."
---

1. Read next week's calendar.
2. Count meetings per day and compare with the person's cap; highlight overloaded days.
3. Flag conflicts, meetings without agenda or link, back-to-back stretches longer than three hours, and missing travel time.
4. List deadlines or commitments mentioned in recent mail that land next week.
5. Suggest specific changes: which meetings to move, shorten or decline, and where to protect focus time.

Output:
## Next week
| Day | Meetings | Hours booked | Flags |
Then: Suggested changes (numbered), Deadlines, Prep needed.
Make no calendar changes until the person approves each one.
