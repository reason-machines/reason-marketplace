---
name: "Find a meeting time"
description: "Use when someone needs to meet with the person and times have to be proposed or a meeting set up."
---

1. Confirm the meeting length, attendees, and any deadline.
2. Read the person's calendar for the window and apply saved preferences: working hours, focus blocks, meeting cap per day, buffers around travel.
3. If other attendees' availability is visible (same organisation), intersect it; otherwise propose times from the person's side only.
4. Pick two or three slots spread across different days.
5. Draft the message with those times and, if Calendly is connected and the person prefers it, their booking link instead.
6. After the person approves a slot, create the event with title, attendees, link and a one-line agenda, only after they confirm.

Output: the proposed slots (with time zones), the draft message, and the event details ready to create.
